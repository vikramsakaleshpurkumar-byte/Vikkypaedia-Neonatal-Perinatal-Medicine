## Study pillars

### Reference population

The table concerns capillary samples from healthy term infants at about two days of age. The table specifies healthy term infants and samples collected at 48 ± 12 hours; population and specimen differences limit direct transfer.

PDF p. 2125

### Developmental trajectory

The same value may occupy different positions in age-specific reference distributions. The table provides developmental group-specific means and percentiles; its footnote also warns about inadequate later sample sizes.

PDF p. 2123

### Unit conversion

171.04 micromoles/L. Multiply the mg/dL value by 17.104: 10 × 17.104 = 171.04 micromoles/L. This is a unit conversion, not a treatment threshold.

PDF p. 2153

### Milliequivalents

2 mEq. mEq = mg × valence / atomic weight; 40 × 2 / 40 = 2. This calculation concerns elemental calcium, not a calcium-salt dose.

PDF p. 2156

### Biomarker age effects

Reference distributions vary by gestation and postnatal age. The appendix explicitly stratifies procalcitonin by both ages and distinguishes medians, ranges and reference intervals.

PDF p. 2132
