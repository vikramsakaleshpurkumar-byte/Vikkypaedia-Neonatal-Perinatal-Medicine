## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant spends weeks in an environment unlike the uterus during rapid neural development. Which implication follows?

A. All sensory exposure is equally beneficial at every age
B. Neural development stops until discharge
C. Only genetic sequence determines all outcomes
D. Physical and social experiences can modify developing neural connections

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Physical and social experiences can modify developing neural connections

Rapid growth, synaptogenesis and plasticity make care experiences capable of supporting or disrupting development.

## Authored MCQ

An immature infant’s sleep does not consistently meet REM or NREM criteria. Which classification is appropriate?

A. A diagnosis of brain death
B. Indeterminate sleep
C. Proof of absent sleep
D. Normal adult wakefulness by definition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Indeterminate sleep

Immature sleep states may not meet established REM or NREM criteria and can be classified as indeterminate.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A very preterm infant shows little crying during a heel stick. Which interpretation is supported?

A. Pain pathways develop only after term birth
B. Pain sensitivity remains despite immature behavioral expression
C. Recurrent painful exposure has no developmental relevance
D. Absence of crying proves absence of pain

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Pain sensitivity remains despite immature behavioral expression

Very preterm infants are pain-sensitive despite weak overt responses; repeated pain is associated with developmental effects.

</details>
