## Authored MCQ

A newborn has ringlike limb constrictions with distal swelling and irregular digital amputations. Which mechanism is most consistent?

A. A uniform isolated delay in bone ossification
B. Disruption associated with amniotic bands
C. A normal postnatal skin fold in every case
D. Typical isolated hip laxity

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Disruption associated with amniotic bands

Bands can constrict appendages, produce distal lymphedema and cause intrauterine amputations or syndactyly.

A neonate has hemivertebrae and a congenital spinal curve. Which associated evaluation is explicitly recommended in the source?

A. A blanket conclusion of normal urinary anatomy
B. Only a visual skin examination without internal assessment
C. Assume the spine finding excludes other organ abnormalities
D. Renal ultrasound for genitourinary malformations

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Genitourinary malformations, cardiac defects and spinal dysraphism may accompany congenital scoliosis; renal ultrasound assesses urinary anomalies.

## Authored MCQ

A trained examiner gently provokes instability with one maneuver and reduces a recently dislocated hip with another. Which mapping is correct?

A. Barlow reduces; Ortolani provokes dislocation
B. Any soft click is equivalent to a definite reduction clunk
C. Both are tests only of tibial rotation
D. Barlow tests dislocatability; Ortolani assesses reduction

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Barlow tests dislocatability; Ortolani assesses reduction

The maneuvers test different aspects of stability; a true shift or clunk must be distinguished from common benign clicks.

</details>

B. Exclude neuromuscular disease solely because the foot finding is present at birth
C. Look for structural rigidity and associated neurologic or syndromic abnormalities
D. Assume every clubfoot has an identical cause and prognosis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Look for structural rigidity and associated neurologic or syndromic abnormalities

The chapter distinguishes congenital, teratologic and positional forms and recommends examination for other abnormalities.
