## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant develops increasing apnea, feeding intolerance, abdominal distension, thrombocytopenia and metabolic acidosis. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

A. Possible NEC requiring integrated clinical, laboratory and imaging evaluation
B. Benign reflux established by thrombocytopenia
C. A specific diagnosis established by feeding intolerance alone
D. Exclusion of bowel disease because early signs are nonspecific

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Possible NEC requiring integrated clinical, laboratory and imaging evaluation

The combination is concerning for intestinal disease and systemic deterioration, although individual signs are nonspecific.

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Stool may mimic pneumatosis and absence of clear pneumatosis does not exclude disease

The source describes both misinterpretation of stool and disease progression without classic gas findings.

## Authored MCQ

A unit reviews nutrition practices for very preterm infants. Which rationale supports promoting mother’s own milk?

A. Its benefit is explained solely by having no bioactive components
B. It is associated with less NEC and provides immune and barrier-supporting components
C. It guarantees that NEC cannot occur
D. It makes assessment of new abdominal signs unnecessary

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

It is associated with less NEC and provides immune and barrier-supporting components

Human milk is associated with reduced NEC and has multiple protective bioactive constituents; risk is not eliminated.
