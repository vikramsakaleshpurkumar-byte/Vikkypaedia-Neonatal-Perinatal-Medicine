## Authored MCQ

A screening examination identifies a white pupillary reflex. What is the most appropriate interpretation?

A. A harmless normal variant in every newborn
B. An important abnormal observation requiring immediate ophthalmologic evaluation
C. A definitive diagnosis of one tumor without examination
D. A finding to ignore until the infant can describe visual symptoms

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An important abnormal observation requiring immediate ophthalmologic evaluation

Leukocoria is a descriptive finding with serious differential diagnoses; prompt assessment is required.

## Authored MCQ

A young infant has tearing, light sensitivity and blepharospasm with a progressively cloudy enlarged cornea. Which process is most concerning?

A. Isolated benign conjunctival pigmentation
B. A normal transient pupillary membrane alone
C. Congenital glaucoma
D. Simple nasolacrimal obstruction without corneal involvement

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Congenital glaucoma

Raised intraocular pressure can stretch the eye and damage vision; tearing, photophobia and blepharospasm are classic symptoms.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has excessive tearing. The clinician suspects nasolacrimal obstruction but has not assessed corneal appearance or light sensitivity. What important alternative requires consideration?

A. Uncomplicated nasolacrimal obstruction as the sole possibility
B. Only absence of the lid puncta
C. Congenital glaucoma
D. Only chronic nasal congestion

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Congenital glaucoma

Nasolacrimal obstruction is common, but glaucoma is a particularly important alternative in the tearing infant.

## Authored MCQ

An infant’s abnormal reflex is localized to an opacity within the lens. Which term describes that finding?

A. Glaucoma solely because the lens is opaque
B. Cataract
C. Nasolacrimal duct obstruction
D. Isolated retinal neovascularization

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Cataract

Cataract denotes lens opacity; neonatal causes include hereditary, metabolic, infectious, traumatic and other conditions.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An infant has an intraocular mass detected after the family noticed a white pupil. Which malignancy is a major consideration?

A. Infantile fibrosarcoma of the forearm
B. Sacrococcygeal teratoma
C. Neuroblastoma arising only within the adrenal gland
D. Retinoblastoma

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Retinoblastoma

Retinoblastoma is the most frequent malignant intraocular tumor in children and may present with leukocoria or strabismus.
