## Authored MCQ

Two infants have the same total calcium but different albumin concentrations. Which measurement most directly reflects the physiologically active calcium fraction?

A. Albumin-bound calcium alone
B. Total serum protein alone
C. The nonionized citrate-bound fraction alone
D. Ionized calcium

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Ionized calcium

Ionized calcium is the active fraction; total calcium also includes protein-bound and complexed fractions.

## Authored MCQ

A term infant develops hypocalcemia near the end of the first week while receiving a high phosphorus load. Which developmental limitation contributes?

A. A necessary increase in renal calcium production
B. Limited renal phosphorus excretion and immature response to PTH
C. Complete absence of phosphorus absorption
D. A mechanism requiring maternal diabetes in every case

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Limited renal phosphorus excretion and immature response to PTH

Immature renal handling and relative PTH resistance can promote phosphate retention and lower calcium.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An infant has persistent neuromuscular irritability and hypocalcemia with a low magnesium concentration. Which relationship is relevant?

A. Low magnesium always produces high calcium
B. Hypomagnesemia can coexist with impaired parathyroid function and hypocalcemia
C. Serum magnesium perfectly measures all body magnesium stores
D. Magnesium has no relationship to parathyroid function

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Hypomagnesemia can coexist with impaired parathyroid function and hypocalcemia

Magnesium deficiency may impair PTH-related calcium regulation; the source describes improvement in calcium and PTH with correction in relevant infants.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A newborn has elevated magnesium after maternal treatment for preeclampsia. Which exposure is most relevant?

A. Vitamin K prophylaxis given to the infant
B. Isolated thyroid hormone replacement
C. Routine maternal iron supplementation
D. Magnesium sulfate

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Magnesium sulfate

Maternal magnesium sulfate can raise fetal and neonatal magnesium; renal function affects clearance.

## Authored MCQ

A very preterm infant later develops reduced bone mineral content. Which prenatal contribution is described?

A. An obligatory excess of skeletal mineral in every preterm baby
B. Loss of late-gestation calcium and phosphorus accretion
C. Completion of all mineral accretion before the first trimester ends
D. Protection from nutritional mineral deficits after early birth

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Loss of late-gestation calcium and phosphorus accretion

Premature delivery interrupts fetal mineral accretion; postnatal nutrition and illness add to risk.
