## Authored MCQ

Which conception mechanism produces dizygotic twins?

A. One ovum is fertilized and never divides
B. Two sperm fertilize two ova
C. One fertilized ovum undergoes early cleavage
D. Maternal cells transform into a second embryo

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Two sperm fertilize two ova

Dizygotic twins result from two separate fertilizations; monozygotic twins arise from early cleavage of one conceptus.

## Authored MCQ

Monochorionic twins show oliguria and low fluid around one fetus and polyuria with excess fluid around the other. Which process fits?

A. A required large hemoglobin difference as the defining abnormality
B. Identical normal fluid production in both sacs
C. Isolated maternal dehydration affecting only one fetus by definition
D. Unbalanced volume transfer through placental anastomoses

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Unbalanced volume transfer through placental anastomoses

Donor hypovolemia produces oliguria and oligohydramnios; recipient overload produces polyuria and polyhydramnios.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

Monochorionic twins have marked intertwin hemoglobin differences due to transfer through tiny vessels. Which diagnosis best matches?

A. Identical erythrocyte mass in both twins
B. A dichorionic process requiring no vascular connections
C. Uncomplicated TTTS defined only by volume imbalance
D. Twin anemia-polycythemia sequence

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Twin anemia-polycythemia sequence

TAPS is characterized by donor anemia and recipient polycythemia, unlike the principal volume imbalance of TTTS.

## Authored MCQ

One monochorionic twin has no functional heart and receives circulation from the other twin. What threatens the pump twin?

A. Loss of all placental vascular connections
B. Heart failure from supplying both circulations
C. Guaranteed cardiovascular protection from extra workload
D. A process confined to maternal circulation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Heart failure from supplying both circulations

The pump twin supports both fetuses through placental connections and may develop congestive heart failure.
