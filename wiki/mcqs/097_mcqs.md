## Authored MCQ

A newborn has a creatinine concentration similar to the mother’s. Which interpretive limitation is important during the first days?

A. Maternal creatinine cannot cross the placenta
B. The birth value measures only steady-state neonatal filtration
C. The first result alone defines the entire renal trajectory
D. The initial value reflects maternal creatinine as well as neonatal transition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The initial value reflects maternal creatinine as well as neonatal transition

Immediately after birth, creatinine reflects the maternal concentration; the postnatal trend and gestation are important.

## Authored MCQ

A bag specimen has bacterial growth in a newborn being evaluated for UTI. What limitation should be addressed?

A. A urinalysis and a culture have identical collection requirements
B. Bag growth always proves an invasive infection
C. The bag method eliminates all contamination
D. Fecal contamination can cause false positives; obtain an appropriate culture specimen

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Fecal contamination can cause false positives; obtain an appropriate culture specimen

A bag can provide urinalysis material but is not recommended for culture confirmation; catheterization is more reliable.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has glucose in urine despite normal serum glucose, together with excessive urinary phosphorus and amino acids. Which mechanism is most consistent?

A. A purely distal urinary obstruction without tubular effects
B. Normal filtration with no transport abnormality
C. Generalized proximal tubular dysfunction
D. Isolated hyperglycemia exceeding the glucose threshold

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Generalized proximal tubular dysfunction

Combined urinary solute losses suggest a proximal tubulopathy such as Fanconi syndrome.

## Authored MCQ

A hypotensive infant initially has reduced kidney perfusion, but dysfunction persists after perfusion improves. Which mechanism becomes a concern?

A. Exclusion of nephrotoxic contributors
B. Immediate proof that every nephron was absent at birth
C. Acute tubular injury after a prolonged prerenal state
D. A normal developmental response requiring no reassessment

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Acute tubular injury after a prolonged prerenal state

Prolonged hypoperfusion can lead to intrinsic tubular injury; perinatal illness and nephrotoxins are relevant risks.

## Authored MCQ

A male newborn has a poor urinary stream, distended thick-walled bladder and bilateral upper-tract dilation. Which study directly assesses the suspected outlet lesion?

A. Voiding cystourethrogram
B. A serum creatinine alone
C. An abdominal radiograph alone
D. An isolated renal function blood test without imaging

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Voiding cystourethrogram

VCUG demonstrates the posterior urethral obstruction and associated reflux; treatment centers on securing urinary drainage.
