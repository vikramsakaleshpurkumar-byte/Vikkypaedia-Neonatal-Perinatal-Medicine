A service proposes routine bed rest to prevent preterm birth. Which appraisal is supported?

A. Benefit is unproven and adverse effects are possible
B. It is equivalent to corticosteroid-mediated lung maturation
C. Bed rest has no possible maternal harm
D. Strong evidence establishes major neonatal benefit

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Benefit is unproven and adverse effects are possible

The source finds no demonstrated benefit and cites thromboembolism, weakness, bone loss and depression as harms.

## Authored MCQ

B. Gain time for corticosteroids, neuroprotection or transfer
C. Guarantee continuation until term
D. Replace all antenatal corticosteroids

The goal has shifted toward a short window, often48 hours, for beneficial interventions and transport.

## Authored MCQ

An antenatal intervention reduces RDS and neonatal mortality in trials of threatened preterm birth. Which intervention is described?

A. Oral magnesium as treatment for preterm labor
B. Maintenance tocolysis as a substitute for fetal maturation therapy
C. Routine maternal bed rest
D. Antenatal glucocorticoids

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Antenatal glucocorticoids

Trials and meta-analysis show reduced RDS, neonatal mortality, IVH and NEC with antenatal glucocorticoids.

PDF p. 357
</details>
