## Authored MCQ

Which observation helped motivate the Barker hypothesis?

A. Adult disease occurring only after large birth size
B. Birth weight predicting every adult diagnosis with certainty
C. Small birth size associated with later cardiometabolic disease
D. No relation between prenatal conditions and later health

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Small birth size associated with later cardiometabolic disease

Replicated epidemiologic associations linked small birth size with later coronary disease, hypertension and metabolic disorders.

## Authored MCQ

A fetus is exposed to an adverse nutritional milieu but has normal birth size. Which conclusion is most defensible?

A. Normal size proves absence of all fetal stress
B. Only calorie deficiency can affect development
C. Normal size does not exclude later programming effects
D. Programming requires a visible malformation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Normal size does not exclude later programming effects

The source describes later disease after nutritional perturbations without a change in size and after several nonnutritional stresses.

## Authored MCQ

An infant develops under constrained nutrition and later lives with energy-dense food and little activity. Which concept describes the disparity?

A. A guarantee that later environment cannot modify risk
B. An effect confined to the prenatal period
C. Mismatch between early developmental cues and later environment
D. A genetic sequence change required in every case

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Mismatch between early developmental cues and later environment

A larger disparity between early adaptation and the later environment can increase disease risk.
