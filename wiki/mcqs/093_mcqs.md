## Authored MCQ

A developmental defect prevents anti-müllerian hormone action despite androgen production. Which structure is most likely to persist because of that specific signaling defect?

A. Müllerian duct structures
B. Only the pancreatic duct
C. Only the ureteric bud
D. Only the wolffian duct because AMH normally maintains it

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Müllerian duct structures

AMH acts locally to induce müllerian regression; testosterone instead supports wolffian differentiation.

## Authored MCQ

A fetus produces testosterone but cannot convert it efficiently in peripheral tissues. Which enzyme pathway is implicated in altered external virilization?

A. Insulin conversion into adrenal androgens
B. Aromatase conversion to estradiol as the same direct pathway
C. AMH conversion into testosterone
D. 5-alpha-reductase conversion to dihydrotestosterone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

5-alpha-reductase conversion to dihydrotestosterone

5-alpha-reductase generates DHT from testosterone in peripheral tissues; DHT is the potent androgen emphasized for external differentiation.

</details>
