## Authored MCQ

A fetal procedure may benefit the fetus but offers no direct maternal health benefit. What must counseling explicitly address?

A. Maternal procedural risks and available alternatives
B. Automatic consent because imaging shows an anomaly
C. A guarantee that maternal morbidity is absent
D. Only the predicted fetal technical success

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Maternal procedural risks and available alternatives

Anesthesia, surgery and continued pregnancy expose the mother to risks, so counseling must cover these and alternatives.

## Authored MCQ

A team plans a percutaneous fetal procedure with limited direct visualization. Which ultrasound assessment most directly supports safe access?

A. Only the volume of the maternal bladder
B. Fetal estimated weight alone
C. Fetal sex and a previous dating measurement alone
D. Placental location, fetal position and maternal vessels

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Placental location, fetal position and maternal vessels

Ultrasound defines the lesion, placental anatomy, fetal position and maternal vessels and supports fetal monitoring.

</details>

A patient previously underwent open fetal surgery. Which source-described concern affects future labor planning?

C. A scar identical in implications to every routine low-segment cesarean scar
D. Uterine rupture risk requiring cesarean delivery in future pregnancies

The source describes mandatory cesarean delivery after fetal-surgery hysterotomy because of rupture risk.

## Authored MCQ

A fetal neck mass is expected to obstruct the airway at birth. Which strategy permits airway intervention before placental support ends?

A. Cord clamping before any airway intervention
B. Amniocentesis alone
C. Postnatal observation without an airway plan
D. EXIT with uteroplacental circulation maintained

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

EXIT with uteroplacental circulation maintained

EXIT maintains uteroplacental circulation during the fetal intervention and is followed by delivery once the airway is established.
