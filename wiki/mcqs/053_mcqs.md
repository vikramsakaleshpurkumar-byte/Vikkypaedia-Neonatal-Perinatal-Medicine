## Authored MCQ

Which process converts the embryonic neural plate into the structure that forms brain and spinal cord?

A. Migration of mature cortical neurons into peripheral nerves
B. Only synapse pruning in adolescence
C. Modeling, folding and closure into the neural tube
D. Only postnatal myelin deposition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Modeling, folding and closure into the neural tube

Neurulation includes plate formation, modeling, groove formation and closure.

## Authored MCQ

Successive waves of cortical neurons migrate past earlier cohorts. Which layer sequence best fits the described developmental pattern?

A. Cortical layers arise only by postnatal neuron division
B. Later neurons occupy progressively more superficial layers
C. Later neurons remain closest to the ventricle in all layers
D. All neurons form one layer simultaneously

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Later neurons occupy progressively more superficial layers

After early deep layers form, later neurons pass them toward superficial cortical positions.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An MRI describes absent gyri in one region and a few broad gyri in another. Which terminology matches?

A. Hydrocephalus and ventriculitis, respectively
B. Agyria and pachygyria, respectively
C. Both terms mean normal age-appropriate cortical folding
D. Pachygyria and agyria, respectively

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Agyria and pachygyria, respectively

Agyria is lack of gyration; pachygyria describes reduced broad gyri with shallow sulci in the lissencephaly spectrum.

PDF p. 1008
</details>
