## Authored MCQ

Which physiologic effect is expected during the first minutes with an intact placental connection?

A. The procedure directly ventilates the lungs
B. All fetal blood must remain in the placenta
C. Every delayed clamp prevents any jaundice
D. More placental blood can return to the infant

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

More placental blood can return to the infant

Delay permits placental transfusion and reduces residual placental blood volume.

## Authored MCQ

Which outcome was reduced at 3–6 months in the cited term evidence?

A. All need for developmental follow-up
B. All neonatal infection
C. Iron-deficiency anemia
D. Every inherited hemoglobin disorder

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Iron-deficiency anemia

Placental transfusion improves early hemoglobin and hematocrit and reduces later infant iron-deficiency anemia.

## Authored MCQ

A provider squeezes an uncut cord toward the infant and allows it to refill between passes. Which method is being used?

A. Cut-cord milking after detachment of a long segment
B. Direct umbilical venous infusion of donor blood
C. Immediate clamping without transfusion
D. Intact cord milking

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Intact cord milking

Intact milking uses the attached uncut cord with repeated refilling; cut-cord milking uses a detached segment.

PDF p. 598
</details>
