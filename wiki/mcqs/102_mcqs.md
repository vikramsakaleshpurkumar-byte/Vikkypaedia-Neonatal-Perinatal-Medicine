## Authored MCQ

A newborn is irritable during handling and moves one limb less, but has no striking fever. What interpretation is safest?

A. Absence of fever excludes infection
B. Skeletal infection remains possible despite subtle systemic findings
C. Normal appearance of another limb excludes focal infection
D. Pseudoparalysis always establishes birth trauma

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Skeletal infection remains possible despite subtle systemic findings

Immature neonatal responses may produce subtle signs; reduced movement, feeding intolerance or irritability can be early clues.

## Authored MCQ

A bacteremic newborn develops infection near a long-bone growth plate. Which anatomic feature contributes?

A. Complete absence of metaphyseal blood flow
B. An avascular joint surface delivering bacteria directly to every bone
C. A necessary penetrating injury in every case
D. Vascular loops and sluggish metaphyseal flow favor bacterial lodging

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Vascular loops and sluggish metaphyseal flow favor bacterial lodging

The metaphyseal vascular arrangement promotes trapping and proliferation during hematogenous spread.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has persistent suspicion of bone infection with nondiagnostic plain radiographs. Which modality can show early bone involvement, abscess and adjacent arthritis?

A. Magnetic resonance imaging
B. Ultrasound alone as a complete marrow assessment
C. Plain radiographs alone despite persistent suspicion
D. A negative bone scan treated as definitive exclusion

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Magnetic resonance imaging

MRI is described as sensitive for early infection and anatomic extent; stability and transfer or sedation needs affect feasibility.

## Authored MCQ

A neonate has a warm, poorly moving hip with an effusion. Which procedure provides material for organism identification and synovial analysis?

A. A superficial skin swab alone
B. Blood cultures alone without joint-fluid sampling
C. Joint aspiration by an experienced clinician
D. Ultrasound demonstration of effusion alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Joint aspiration by an experienced clinician

Aspiration permits culture, Gram stain and synovial fluid assessment; blood cultures are also obtained.

## Authored MCQ

Purulent fluid accumulates in a neonatal hip. What consequence makes prompt treatment essential?

A. Cartilage damage and pressure-related compromise of epiphyseal blood supply
B. Only a transient skin-color change without joint effects
C. Guaranteed harmless spontaneous sterilization
D. Protection of all growth structures by the joint capsule

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Cartilage damage and pressure-related compromise of epiphyseal blood supply

Pus and inflammation damage cartilage; increased pressure can compromise epiphyseal perfusion and cause permanent injury.
