## Authored MCQ

An infant has an adrenal-region mass suspected to arise from neural crest-derived sympathetic tissue. Which urine measurements support evaluation?

A. LDH and uric acid alone
B. Homovanillic acid and vanillylmandelic acid
C. Cortisol and ACTH alone
D. Alpha-fetoprotein and beta-hCG

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Homovanillic acid and vanillylmandelic acid

Many neuroblastomas secrete catecholamines; urinary HVA and VMA are commonly used diagnostic tests.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn with a suspected germ-cell tumor has AFP above the adult range. Which interpretation is appropriate?

A. AFP is normally absent in every newborn
B. Any value above the adult range proves malignancy
C. AFP is physiologically high at birth and must be assessed against age-appropriate values
D. A single neonatal AFP value excludes all malignant components

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

AFP is physiologically high at birth and must be assessed against age-appropriate values

Normal AFP is high at birth and declines over infancy; tumor assessment requires developmental context and the broader findings.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A neonatal leukemia workup identifies a recurrent rearrangement historically termed MLL. What is the modern gene name described?

A. CFTR
B. RB1
C. KMT2A
D. SLC26A3

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

KMT2A

MLL is now described as KMT2A; rearrangements are common in congenital and infant acute leukemias.
