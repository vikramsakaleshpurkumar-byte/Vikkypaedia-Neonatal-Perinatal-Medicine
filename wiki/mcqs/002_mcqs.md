## Authored MCQ

Two exposures each have a risk ratio of 2. One changes risk from 0.1% to 0.2%, the other from 10% to 20%. Which interpretation is best?

A. Absolute increases are identical because both ratios are 2
B. The first exposure necessarily has the larger absolute effect
C. Relative effects match, but absolute differences differ
D. Neither comparison can have a measurable absolute difference

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Relative effects match, but absolute differences differ

The absolute increases are 0.1 and 10 percentage points. Equal relative effects need not have equal clinical impact.

## Authored MCQ

A cohort associates one exposure with a prematurity morbidity. Why should the clinician avoid concluding that this is the sole cause?

A. All relevant causes are measured in every cohort
B. Every association is necessarily causal
C. Only one exposure can contribute to each morbidity
D. Multiple component causes and their sequence may contribute

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Multiple component causes and their sequence may contribute

The text describes IVH, NEC and BPD as multifactorial, with causes that can be unknown, unmeasured or time dependent.

## Authored MCQ

A service studies maternal and infant mortality together with access to care. Which discipline directly frames this population-level work?

A. Individual pharmacokinetic modeling alone
B. Histologic diagnosis alone
C. Single-patient ventilator mechanics alone
D. Epidemiology

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Epidemiology

Epidemiology studies population health and disease, risk factors and associations, including mortality as an indicator of health and care.

## Authored MCQ

After complete delivery, an infant has a beating heart but has not yet breathed. Which feature supports classification as a live birth in the stated definition?

A. Placental separation as a prerequisite
B. A minimum birth weight regardless of signs of life
C. Cutting the umbilical cord as a prerequisite
D. A definite sign of life such as heartbeat

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A definite sign of life such as heartbeat

Breathing or another definite sign of life after delivery qualifies; cord cutting and placental separation are not prerequisites.

A regional comparison reports differing maternal and infant mortality rates. What is an appropriate use of these indicators?

A. Assess population health, access and quality in context
B. Diagnose the precise cause of death in every individual
C. Replace examination of definitions and data sources
D. Attribute every difference to one clinician

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Assess population health, access and quality in context

Mortality rates inform assessment of population health and care systems, but interpretation requires context and consistent definitions.
