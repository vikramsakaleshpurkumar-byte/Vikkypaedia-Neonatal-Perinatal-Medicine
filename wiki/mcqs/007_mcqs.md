## Authored MCQ

A newborn has a core temperature of 36.2°C. Under the chapter’s stated definition, how should this be classified?

A. Hypothermia because it is below 36.5°C
B. Normal solely because the ambient season is warm
C. Hyperthermia because newborns should be cooler
D. A finding irrelevant to neonatal risk

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Hypothermia because it is below 36.5°C

The chapter defines neonatal hypothermia below 36.5°C and notes that it can occur even in warm seasons.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn is bathed early, lightly clothed and transferred through a cool environment. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

A. Transport has no effect on thermal care
B. Term newborns are never vulnerable to cold
C. These practices can contribute to preventable heat loss
D. Routine early bathing prevents every episode of hypothermia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

These practices can contribute to preventable heat loss

Early bathing, inadequate clothing and losses during transport are described causes of neonatal hypothermia.

## Authored MCQ

A. Thermoregulation, nutrition and sepsis prevention/treatment
B. Respiratory technology removes every other care need
C. Nutrition can always wait until respiratory disease resolves
D. Warmth is needed only for term infants

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Thermoregulation, nutrition and sepsis prevention/treatment

The chapter emphasizes several effective care domains; a respiratory intervention does not replace thermal, nutritional and infection care.
