## Authored MCQ

Which feature most directly justifies adapting anesthetic planning for a preterm neonate?

A. Adult-equivalent physiology at a smaller weight
B. No need for adapted equipment
C. Immature organ systems and altered drug handling
D. Uniform metabolism across all gestations

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Immature organ systems and altered drug handling

Anatomy, physiology, homeostasis and pharmacology differ between adults, term neonates and preterm neonates.

## Authored MCQ

Which combination should be anticipated in neonatal airway management?

A. Relatively large head and tongue with a short neck and higher anterior larynx
B. Long neck with a proportionally tiny occiput
C. An airway identical to an adult after weight adjustment
D. Small tongue with an adult-position larynx

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Relatively large head and tongue with a short neck and higher anterior larynx

Neonatal proportions affect positioning and airway management; these differences extend beyond size.

## Authored MCQ

A neonate under anesthesia develops bradycardia. Which physiologic limitation contributes to reduced cardiac output?

A. Complete independence of output from heart rate
B. Unlimited stroke-volume reserve
C. A requirement for very low heart rates to sustain output
D. Limited ability to augment stroke volume

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Limited ability to augment stroke volume

The neonatal heart has limited stroke-volume reserve, making cardiac output strongly dependent on heart rate.

## Authored MCQ

During surgery a neonate is exposed and receives general anesthesia. Which mechanism worsens thermal vulnerability?

A. Blunted thermoregulation plus cutaneous vasodilation
B. Complete protection from exposed body cavities
C. Effective adult-like shivering as the main defense
D. Improved nonshivering thermogenesis in every case

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Blunted thermoregulation plus cutaneous vasodilation

Anesthesia interferes with thermal defenses and increases heat loss; open cavities add risk.

## Authored MCQ

A low-birth-weight infant fasts for a prolonged operation. Which issue supports glucose monitoring?

A. Anesthesia makes clinical jitteriness fully reliable
B. Abundant glycogen guarantees normoglycemia
C. Only adults require assessment of glucose during fasting
D. Limited reserves and masked clinical signs under anesthesia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Limited reserves and masked clinical signs under anesthesia

Neonates have high glucose demand and limited reserves, while anesthesia masks usual signs.
