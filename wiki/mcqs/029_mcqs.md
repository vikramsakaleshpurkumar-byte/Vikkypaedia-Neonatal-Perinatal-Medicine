## Authored MCQ

A structural abnormality is present at birth. What can be concluded from the word congenital alone?

A. The feature is present at birth, with genetic or nongenetic causes possible
B. The cause must be an inherited single-gene disorder
C. Every parent must carry the same mutation
D. Environmental contributions are excluded

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The feature is present at birth, with genetic or nongenetic causes possible

Congenital denotes presence at birth; it does not specify cause.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has a structural defect requiring substantial surgical intervention. Which classification fits?

A. Minor anomaly solely because it was detected early
B. Major anomaly
C. Normal variant by definition
D. A finding that proves one specific genotype

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Major anomaly

Major anomalies have significant surgical or cosmetic implications; minor anomalies do not have that significance.

## Authored MCQ

Several subtle anomalies are observed in a newborn. What is a justified next reasoning step?

A. Assume minor findings can never offer clues
B. Diagnose a syndrome from one common palmar crease alone
C. Look for a characteristic pattern and associated major malformations
D. Ignore familial or population variation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Look for a characteristic pattern and associated major malformations

Minor findings may be normal variants but combinations can identify patterns or associated major anomalies.

## Authored MCQ

Microarray detects a deletion of uncertain relationship to the phenotype. What additional information may help?

A. Assuming a small variant is always harmless
B. Assuming every detected variant is the cause
C. Discarding the clinical phenotype once a laboratory result exists
D. Whether it is de novo or inherited, alongside phenotype assessment

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Whether it is de novo or inherited, alongside phenotype assessment

Microarray can identify variants not responsible for the phenotype; inheritance information aids interpretation.
