## Authored MCQ

A team is deciding whether an intervention benefits a critically ill infant. Which approach best represents ethical deliberation?

A. Reasoned evaluation of benefits, burdens and values
B. Choosing solely by technical feasibility
C. Excluding parents because the decision is difficult
D. Assuming every question has a simple universally correct answer

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Reasoned evaluation of benefits, burdens and values

Ethical reasoning evaluates and justifies action in pursuit of well-being, while recognizing uncertainty and differing values.

## Authored MCQ

A parent accurately repeats the survival statistics but remains unable to deliberate. What should the clinician recognize?

A. Correct recall means no further support is needed
B. Emotional responses establish incapacity by themselves
C. Emotions and lived experience can shape how information is used
D. More statistics always resolve value conflicts

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Emotions and lived experience can shape how information is used

Parents may use affective and experience-based reasoning; understanding facts alone does not complete supportive communication.
