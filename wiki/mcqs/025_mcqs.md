## Authored MCQ

A sick newborn is transferred after a complicated pregnancy. Which additional investigation may identify treatable processes and underlying mechanisms?

A. Birth weight as the sole etiologic investigation
B. A guarantee of normal placental function from live birth
C. Placental examination
D. Discarding all obstetric information after transfer

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Placental examination

Placental findings can identify infection risk, explain adverse outcomes and inform recurrence assessment.

## Authored MCQ

Placental examination shows low weight, syncytial knotting and villous changes associated with reduced maternal inflow. Which process fits?

A. Exclusive fetal red-cell alloimmunization
B. Maternal vascular malperfusion
C. A lesion proving a specific neonatal pathogen
D. A normal consequence of every healthy placenta

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Maternal vascular malperfusion

Stenosis of maternal arteries supplying the intervillous space produces maternal vascular malperfusion patterns.

Which cord component normally cushions vessels against compression?

B. Maternal erythrocytes surrounding each vessel
C. Hydrated extracellular matrix called Wharton jelly
D. A rigid bony sheath

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Wharton jelly is a hydrated matrix protecting the fetal cord vessels.

## Authored MCQ

A placenta has acute neutrophilic membrane inflammation. Which interpretation avoids overclaiming?

A. Every exposed newborn is bacteremic at birth
B. The finding identifies one specific organism in every case
C. The placenta cannot have any barrier function
D. It is relevant infection-risk evidence but does not prove neonatal sepsis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

It is relevant infection-risk evidence but does not prove neonatal sepsis

The source notes that few infants are septic at birth despite acute chorioamnionitis, consistent with placental protection.
