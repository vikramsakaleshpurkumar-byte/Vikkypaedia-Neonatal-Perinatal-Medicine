## Authored MCQ

A developmental specimen shows extensive conducting-airway branching before a mature gas-exchange barrier is present. Which stage fits?

A. Only the mature postnatal alveolar stage
B. Pseudoglandular stage
C. Only the final adult repair stage
D. A phase after all pulmonary capillaries have disappeared

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Pseudoglandular stage

The pseudoglandular stage establishes the branching airway pattern and associated vascular pathways.

## Authored MCQ

Which developmental combination best explains increasing potential viability of the lung?

A. Acinar formation, thinning air-blood barrier and emerging type II-cell surfactant synthesis
B. Completion of lifelong alveolarization before birth
C. Conducting-airway branching alone without vascular proximity
D. Loss of all pulmonary vessels

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Acinar formation, thinning air-blood barrier and emerging type II-cell surfactant synthesis

Airspace and vascular approximation plus epithelial differentiation begin to establish gas-exchange capacity.

Cuboidal epithelial cells develop cytoplasmic lamellar bodies. Which function is being established?

C. Production of circulating maternal antibodies
D. Surfactant storage and secretion

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Lamellar bodies are characteristic organelles of surfactant-producing type II epithelial cells.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn has delayed clearance of fetal lung liquid. Which transport process is particularly relevant?

A. Sodium absorption through epithelial sodium channels
B. Permanent closure of the pulmonary circulation
C. Placental transfer of all retained lung liquid
D. Continued chloride secretion as the only clearance mechanism

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Sodium absorption through epithelial sodium channels

Active sodium transport promotes absorption; developmental and labor-related signals influence the transition from secretion to clearance.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An immature lung has deficient surfactant and uneven inflation during positive pressure. Which mechanism contributes?

A. Higher surface tension promotes collapse and heterogeneous expansion
B. Surface forces have no effect on small airways
C. Deficiency guarantees uniform expansion of all airspaces
D. Surfactant normally increases collapse pressure

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Higher surface tension promotes collapse and heterogeneous expansion

Low surface tension helps prevent alveolar collapse and fluid entry; deficiency produces uneven recruitment and overdistention.

PDF p. 1204
</details>
