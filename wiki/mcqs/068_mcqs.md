## Authored MCQ

Airflow stops while chest-wall respiratory effort continues. Which pattern is present?

A. Pure central apnea
B. Normal continuous ventilation
C. Obstructive apnea
D. A definition of bradycardia alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Obstructive apnea

Obstruction preserves effort against a closed airway; central apnea has absent respiratory effort.

## Authored MCQ

A monitor continues to register chest movement despite absent airflow. Which limitation is illustrated?

A. Chest movement does not establish airway patency or ventilation
B. A detected movement excludes airway obstruction
C. Impedance directly measures every breath’s gas exchange
D. Oxygenation cannot change during an obstructive event

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Chest movement does not establish airway patency or ventilation

Impedance monitoring detects movement and central pauses but can miss obstruction while effort continues.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A convalescing preterm infant develops more apnea and reduced feeding. Which approach is supported?

A. Dismiss events because they are shorter than 20 seconds despite desaturation
B. Assume immaturity explains every new event
C. Increase caffeine without examining for illness
D. Investigate infection, NEC and other new causes

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Investigate infection, NEC and other new causes

New or increased apnea can signal sepsis or NEC and requires contextual assessment.

## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant has both reflux and apnea, without demonstrated temporal association. Which conclusion matches the source?

A. Do not routinely use acid suppression to treat apnea
B. Coexistence proves reflux causes all apnea
C. Acid suppression is harmless and always prevents bradycardia
D. Every apnea episode requires antireflux surgery

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Do not routinely use acid suppression to treat apnea

Temporal linkage is uncommon and routine reflux treatment has not shown apnea benefit; acid suppression can add infectious and NEC risks.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant’s respiratory drive improves with caffeine. Which established mechanism contributes?

A. Complete substitution for evaluating new illness
B. Antagonism of adenosine receptors
C. Activation of adenosine-mediated inhibition
D. Permanent paralysis of airway muscles

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Antagonism of adenosine receptors

Methylxanthines antagonize adenosine receptors and improve ventilation and responsiveness to carbon dioxide.
