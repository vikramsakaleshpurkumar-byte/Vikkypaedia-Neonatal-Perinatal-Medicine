A well-dated pregnancy reaches42 completed weeks. Which category applies?

A. Early term
B. Late preterm
C. Full term at its lowest-risk interval
D. Post-term

Post-term

Post-term begins at 42 completed weeks or294 days from the last menstrual period.

## Authored MCQ

The reported last menstrual period is uncertain because cycles are irregular. Which dating information is generally more accurate?

A. Third-trimester size alone
B. First-trimester ultrasound
C. Recall of an irregular menstrual period alone
D. Newborn size before delivery

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

First-trimester ultrasound

Early ultrasound improves dating relative to menstrual recall and later pregnancy biometry.

## Authored MCQ

A post-term fetus has low fluid and recurrent variable decelerations. Which mechanism connects these findings?

A. Umbilical cord compression
B. Increased cushioning from excess fluid
C. Proof of fetal chromosome imbalance
D. A required primary fetal tachyarrhythmia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Umbilical cord compression

Reduced fluid increases vulnerability to cord compression and associated fetal heart rate abnormalities.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn after a prolonged gestation has dry cracked skin and little vernix. Which source-described pattern fits?

A. Proof that meconium aspiration has occurred
B. Classic generalized hydrops alone
C. The defining triad of HELLP syndrome
D. Postmaturity syndrome

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Postmaturity syndrome

The classic syndrome includes absent vernix, dry cracked skin and an alert, aged appearance.

## Authored MCQ

Two otherwise similar patients are being counseled about induction. Which finding generally improves the prospect of successful induction?

A. A less favorable closed thick cervix
B. A more dilated and effaced cervix
C. Parity and cervical findings having no relevance
D. An unengaged presenting part as a universal advantage

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A more dilated and effaced cervix

A favorable cervix, engaged presenting part and parity are associated with greater success.
