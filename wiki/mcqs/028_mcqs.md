## Authored MCQ

Which injury belongs within the chapter’s definition of birth injury?

A. A childhood fall months after birth
B. An injury caused by later neonatal resuscitation
C. An injury sustained during labor or delivery
D. A complication of prenatal amniocentesis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An injury sustained during labor or delivery

The source defines birth injury around labor and delivery and excludes prenatal procedure and neonatal resuscitation injuries.

## Authored MCQ

A vertex-born infant has a vaguely demarcated edematous area over the presenting scalp. Which lesion fits?

A. A clavicular fracture
B. An intracerebral hemorrhage established by scalp appearance
C. Caput succedaneum
D. A subperiosteal cephalhematoma by definition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Caput succedaneum

Caput is an edematous swelling over the presenting part of the scalp.

A. Subgaleal hemorrhage
B. Subconjunctival hemorrhage
C. Cephalhematoma
D. Caput succedaneum

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

## Authored MCQ

After vacuum delivery, blood is suspected in the plane between the galea aponeurotica and skull periosteum. What is the lesion?

A. Caput defined only by edema of the presenting part
B. An isolated skull fracture without hemorrhage
C. Subgaleal hemorrhage
D. Cephalhematoma beneath the periosteum

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Subgaleal hemorrhage

Subgaleal hemorrhage occupies the space beneath the galea and above the periosteum and is associated with instrumental delivery.

PDF p. 493
</details>
