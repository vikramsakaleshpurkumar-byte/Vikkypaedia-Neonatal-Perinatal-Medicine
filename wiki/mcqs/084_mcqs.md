## Authored MCQ

An extremely premature infant requires transfusion. Which feature best explains the need for careful metabolic monitoring?

A. Absence of glucose or electrolyte effects from blood products
B. Adult-equivalent clearance of every preservative
C. Complete independence from gestation and illness severity
D. Immature handling of product solutes with a small circulating blood volume

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Immature handling of product solutes with a small circulating blood volume

Immature organ function and limited blood volume increase susceptibility to glucose, potassium and calcium disturbances.

## Authored MCQ

During an exchange transfusion, an infant develops a falling ionized calcium concentration. Which product-related mechanism is most relevant?

A. A compulsory increase in calcium release from citrate
B. A finding that cannot occur during blood-component therapy
C. A loss of all calcium exclusively through the skin
D. Citrate-associated calcium binding with limited neonatal citrate handling

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Citrate-associated calcium binding with limited neonatal citrate handling

Large citrate loads may cause symptomatic hypocalcemia, especially with immature metabolism; exchange differs from a small-volume transfusion.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A seronegative premature infant is being assessed for transfusion-transmitted infection risk. In which donor cells does CMV remain latent?

A. Leukocytes
B. Hemoglobin molecules
C. Mature red cells as the principal latent reservoir
D. Platelet-free plasma proteins alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Leukocytes

CMV remains latent in donor leukocytes and may be transmitted in cellular components; vulnerability is greatest in selected high-risk recipients.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A team assumes a higher prophylactic platelet threshold must reduce major bleeding in premature infants. Which trial result challenges that assumption?

A. Higher thresholds eliminated all major bleeding
B. The higher-threshold group had more new major bleeding or death
C. Threshold choice had no measured outcomes
D. The trial studied only adults with active massive hemorrhage

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The higher-threshold group had more new major bleeding or death

In the cited premature-infant trial, the more liberal threshold was associated with more major bleeding or death; the result must be interpreted in its studied context.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A neonate has an acquired coagulation-factor deficiency with clinically important bleeding. Which component principally supplies multiple coagulation factors?

A. Platelets alone
B. Albumin alone
C. Packed red cells alone
D. Plasma

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Plasma

Plasma supplies factors and is used for selected acquired deficiencies, including DIC or liver-related coagulopathy.

</details>
