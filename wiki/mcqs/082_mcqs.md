## Authored MCQ

A neutrophil count is below an adult reference interval. What is needed before labeling the newborn neutropenic?

A. An appropriate neonatal age and gestation reference distribution
B. Total white-cell count without a differential
C. The adult lower limit alone
D. An assumption that all newborn counts are constant

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An appropriate neonatal age and gestation reference distribution

Neonatal neutrophil values change rapidly and definitions depend on the relevant reference distribution.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has recurrent bacterial and fungal infections despite preserved neutrophil numbers; testing shows failure of the respiratory burst. Which defect fits?

A. NADPH oxidase dysfunction
B. CD18-related failure of neutrophil adhesion
C. Absent effective T-cell development
D. Isolated failure of antibody production

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

NADPH oxidase dysfunction

CGD impairs oxidative microbial killing through defective NADPH oxidase in phagocytes.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has delayed cord separation, recurrent severe bacterial infection, marked leukocytosis and little pus. What mechanism best fits?

A. Normal tissue recruitment with excessive antibody production
B. Impaired neutrophil adhesion and migration into tissues
C. An isolated platelet number defect
D. Isolated reduction in hemoglobin synthesis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Impaired neutrophil adhesion and migration into tissues

LAD impairs trafficking; neutrophils remain in blood rather than accumulating effectively at infected tissues.

## Authored MCQ

A young infant has recurrent infections across bacterial, viral and fungal categories with profound lymphocyte dysfunction. Which approach matches the source?

A. Treat suspected severe combined immunodeficiency as an urgent specialist problem
B. Wait until repeated severe infections establish permanent damage
C. Assume maternal antibody ensures complete long-term protection
D. Reassure because B cells can sometimes be present

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Treat suspected severe combined immunodeficiency as an urgent specialist problem

SCID is described as a pediatric emergency; preserved B-cell numbers in some forms do not establish effective antibody function.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A neonate has persistent fever, splenomegaly, cytopenias, very high ferritin and low fibrinogen. Which syndrome merits evaluation?

A. Hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis
B. Isolated vitamin K deficiency
C. Uncomplicated transient neonatal neutrophilia
D. Isolated physiologic anemia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis

HLH combines systemic inflammation, cytopenias and characteristic biochemical and immune abnormalities; neonatal cases are rare.

</details>
