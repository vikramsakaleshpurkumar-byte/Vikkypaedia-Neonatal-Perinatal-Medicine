## Authored MCQ

Diabetes is documented before conception. Which classification is appropriate?

A. A neonatal metabolic disorder
B. Gestational diabetes first recognized in this pregnancy
C. Pregestational diabetes
D. Transient neonatal hyperinsulinism

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Pregestational diabetes

Pregestational diabetes precedes conception; gestational diabetes is recognized during pregnancy.

## Authored MCQ

A screening program tests only patients with recognized historical risk factors. Which limitation motivated broader screening?

B. Every GDM patient has an obvious prior risk factor
C. Family history alone establishes GDM
D. Many affected patients have no identified risk factor

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The source describes substantial missed cases among women without historical risk factors.

## Authored MCQ

A patient with known diabetes seeks counseling before pregnancy. Why address glucose control now?

A. Organogenesis occurs early, often before later pregnancy treatment begins
B. Congenital anomaly risk begins only during labor
C. Glycemic exposure cannot affect early development
D. Late fetal hyperinsulinemia explains every embryonic anomaly

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Organogenesis occurs early, often before later pregnancy treatment begins

The relevant malformation window is early organogenesis, so preconception control is important.

## Authored MCQ

After cord clamping, a newborn exposed to maternal hyperglycemia develops low glucose. What mechanism best explains this?

A. Immediate cessation of all neonatal insulin secretion
B. Continued unlimited maternal glucose delivery
C. Universal inability to store any prenatal glycogen
D. Persistent increased insulin after maternal glucose supply stops

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Persistent increased insulin after maternal glucose supply stops

Insulin production remains upregulated after separation from the maternal nutrient source.

## Authored MCQ

A patient no longer needs insulin after delivery. Which counseling point remains relevant?

A. Only neonatal follow-up is needed
B. GDM never recurs or predicts metabolic disease
C. Future diabetes risk remains and glucose reassessment is needed
D. Stopping insulin proves lifelong normal glucose tolerance

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Future diabetes risk remains and glucose reassessment is needed

Insulin needs often fall postpartum, but impaired glucose metabolism and later type 2 diabetes remain important risks.

</details>
