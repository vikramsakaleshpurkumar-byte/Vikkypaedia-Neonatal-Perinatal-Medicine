## Authored MCQ

An RhD-negative mother becomes sensitized to RhD-positive erythrocytes. Which process can injure an antigen-positive fetus?

A. Rh status changes the fetal chromosome number
B. Maternal erythrocytes must replace the entire fetal circulation
C. Maternal antibodies cross the placenta and hemolyze fetal erythrocytes
D. Fetal antibodies must first destroy maternal bone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Maternal antibodies cross the placenta and hemolyze fetal erythrocytes

Maternal anti-Rh antibodies can cross the placenta and cause fetal red-cell destruction.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A fetus develops hydrops without a demonstrated immune red-cell cause. Which mechanism could account for fluid accumulation?

A. A requirement for increased plasma oncotic pressure
B. Reduced lymphatic return, reduced oncotic pressure or increased venous pressure
C. Only RhD sensitization by definition
D. Only excess maternal dietary salt in every case

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Reduced lymphatic return, reduced oncotic pressure or increased venous pressure

Imbalance of interstitial production and lymphatic return can arise through several pressure and drainage disturbances.

PDF p. 404
</details>
