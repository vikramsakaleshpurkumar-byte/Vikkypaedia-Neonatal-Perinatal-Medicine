## Authored MCQ

An embryology diagram shows the alimentary tube forming during early folding. Which tissue combination best fits the source description?

A. Surface ectoderm and somites alone
B. Neural tube and notochord alone
C. Endoderm and splanchnic mesoderm
D. Placental trophoblast alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Endoderm and splanchnic mesoderm

Folding of endoderm and splanchnic mesoderm produces the early gut tube.

## Authored MCQ

Normal fixation places the duodenojejunal junction far from the cecum. Why is that arrangement protective?

A. It removes the superior mesenteric blood supply
B. It creates a broad mesenteric base that resists twisting
C. It narrows the vascular pedicle to a single mobile point
D. It prevents all possible gastrointestinal obstruction

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

It creates a broad mesenteric base that resists twisting

Widely separated fixation points broaden the mesenteric base and reduce twisting risk.
