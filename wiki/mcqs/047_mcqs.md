## Authored MCQ

A drug’s concentration falls exponentially and its half-life is independent of dose within the studied range. Which pattern fits?

A. A constant amount eliminated regardless of concentration
B. Half-life necessarily increasing with each dose
C. No elimination until a threshold concentration is reached
D. A constant fraction eliminated per unit time

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A constant fraction eliminated per unit time

First-order elimination removes a constant proportion over time, producing an exponential concentration decrease.

## Authored MCQ

A hydropic infant receives a water-soluble drug. Which change can lower the initial plasma peak at a given mg/kg dose?

A. An expanded volume of distribution
B. A necessarily smaller volume of distribution
C. A contracted extracellular water space
D. Complete blockade of renal elimination as the only explanation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An expanded volume of distribution

Excess extracellular fluid can expand the distribution space for hydrophilic drugs.

## Authored MCQ

A neonate’s urine output falls, but creatinine has not yet risen. Which interpretation best protects against drug accumulation?

A. Renal clearance may already be reduced before creatinine rises
B. All renally eliminated drugs retain unchanged clearance
C. Urine output has no relevance to drug disposition
D. Creatinine rises before any renal functional change

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Renal clearance may already be reduced before creatinine rises

Creatinine takes time to accumulate, so falling filtration can precede a measurable rise.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A cooled infant receives a narrow-therapeutic-index drug cleared by kidney and liver. Which approach is supported?

A. Monitor exposure when available and reassess through cooling and rewarming
B. Stop considering organ recovery once the first dose is given
C. Use an adult regimen unchanged throughout
D. Assume temperature cannot alter clearance

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Monitor exposure when available and reassess through cooling and rewarming

Temperature, perfusion and recovery of organ function can change exposure over the treatment course.

## Authored MCQ

Two ampicillin regimens have similar peaks but different time above the organism’s MIC. Which feature is most directly relevant to its time-dependent activity?

A. Time above MIC during the dosing interval
B. The highest trough regardless of organism susceptibility
C. Peak concentration alone irrespective of duration
D. Total administered volume independent of concentration

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Time above MIC during the dosing interval

Beta-lactam efficacy is associated with the duration that concentrations exceed the MIC.
