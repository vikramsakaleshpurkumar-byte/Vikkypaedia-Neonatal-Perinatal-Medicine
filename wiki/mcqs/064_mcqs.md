## Authored MCQ

A newborn exhales through partially closed vocal cords. What physiologic effect can this produce?

A. Complete elimination of all airway pressure
B. A guaranteed increase in lung compliance without disease treatment
C. Evidence that intubation cannot alter lung volume
D. Expiratory braking that helps preserve functional residual capacity

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Expiratory braking that helps preserve functional residual capacity

Partial glottic closure retains gas and increases expiratory pressure; after intubation PEEP helps replace this mechanism.

## Authored MCQ

An infant develops gas trapping and rising PaCO2 during support. Which mechanism can contribute?

A. Increased effective alveolar ventilation in every region
B. Diffusion limitation as the usual dominant neonatal CO2 mechanism
C. A mandatory decrease in CO2 production to zero
D. Increased dead space

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Increased dead space

Gas trapping and overdistention can increase ineffective ventilation and reduce CO2 elimination.

## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant on supplemental oxygen has a saturation at the top of the dissociation curve. Which limitation matters?

A. Large changes in PaO2 may produce little change in saturation
B. A saturation near 100% proves PaO2 is never excessive
C. Pulse oximetry measures inspired oxygen concentration
D. Saturation directly measures PaCO2

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Large changes in PaO2 may produce little change in saturation

The flat upper dissociation curve limits discrimination of high arterial oxygen tensions.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A relaxed respiratory system shows a smaller volume change for the same pressure change. What has decreased?

A. The inspired oxygen fraction by definition
B. Resistance defined only by pressure divided by flow
C. Elastance
D. Compliance

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Compliance

Compliance relates volume change to pressure change; a stiffer system has lower compliance and greater elastance.

PDF p. 1219
</details>

## Authored MCQ

Two lung regions have different resistance and compliance. Why may they fill or empty at different rates?

A. Their resistance-compliance products differ
B. Compliance affects volume but can never affect timing
C. Time constants are identical in all lung regions
D. Only oxygen concentration determines equilibration time

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Their resistance-compliance products differ

The time constant reflects resistance multiplied by compliance, with heterogeneous mechanics affecting equilibration.

</details>
