## Authored MCQ

New hypertension is detected in a previously normotensive pregnant patient. Why is later reassessment still needed?

A. It always resolves before delivery
B. It may resolve, progress to preeclampsia or reveal chronic hypertension
C. One measurement establishes lifelong hypertension
D. It can never progress to another hypertensive disorder

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

It may resolve, progress to preeclampsia or reveal chronic hypertension

The group includes transient disease, evolving preeclampsia and previously unrecognized chronic hypertension.

## Authored MCQ

A pregnant patient has hemolysis, increased liver transaminases and thrombocytopenia. Which pregnancy-associated syndrome is defined by this triad?

A. Gestational hypertension
B. Chronic hypertension
C. HELLP syndrome
D. Eclampsia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

HELLP syndrome

HELLP denotes hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes and low platelets; hypertension or proteinuria need not both be present.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A patient initially lacks severe features. Which statement best supports continued close monitoring?

A. Absence of severe features guarantees stability
B. Progression may be unpredictable and rapid
C. Proteinuria severity alone predicts the complete course
D. Preeclampsia cannot first present postpartum

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Progression may be unpredictable and rapid

Progression can occur within hours to days, and postpartum presentation is also possible.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A preterm patient has preeclampsia. Which framework addresses the treatment trade-off?

A. Gestational age, disease severity and maternal-fetal well-being
B. Proteinuria quantity as the only variable
C. Deliver at one identical gestation regardless of disease
D. Delay delivery in every case to reach term

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Gestational age, disease severity and maternal-fetal well-being

Delivery treats the maternal disease, but prematurity creates fetal risks; timing requires integrated assessment.

PDF p. 312
</details>

## Authored MCQ

A patient with severe preeclampsia receives intrapartum magnesium sulfate. What is the principal purpose in this context?

A. Replacement of all blood-pressure surveillance
B. Definitive removal of the placental cause
C. Correction of fetal chromosome abnormalities
D. Prevention of eclamptic seizures

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Prevention of eclamptic seizures

Magnesium sulfate is supported for seizure prophylaxis in severe preeclampsia.
