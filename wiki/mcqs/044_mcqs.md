## Authored MCQ

A fetus has a life-limiting condition and parents need support making decisions. Which service goal best fits their needs?

A. Optimize comfort and quality of life while supporting family decisions
B. Replace family values with a predetermined plan
C. Provide care only after every treatment has stopped
D. Offer only analgesic prescriptions without family support

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Optimize comfort and quality of life while supporting family decisions

Palliative care coordinates multidisciplinary support for infant comfort and caregiver needs throughout the decision process.

## Authored MCQ

After a goals discussion, a dying newborn develops distressing air hunger. Which principle guides treatment?

A. Withhold symptom relief because cure is impossible
B. Relieve dyspnea using the comfort plan, including appropriate opioids
C. Escalate invasive support solely to normalize every monitor value
D. Use oxygen saturation alone as the measure of comfort

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Relieve dyspnea using the comfort plan, including appropriate opioids

For end-of-life dyspnea the goal is comfort; opioids are described as a mainstay of symptom relief.
