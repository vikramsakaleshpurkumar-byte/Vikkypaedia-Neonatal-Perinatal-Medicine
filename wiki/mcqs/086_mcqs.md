## Authored MCQ

A thriving infant regurgitates after feeds without a demonstrated complication. Which distinction is essential before labeling this a disease?

A. GERD requires medical complications attributable to reflux
B. No healthy infant has reflux
C. Any reflux event establishes GERD
D. Regurgitation is required in every GERD case

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

GERD requires medical complications attributable to reflux

GER is retrograde flow; GERD denotes a pathologic condition causing complications. Regurgitation alone is insufficient.

## Authored MCQ

Manometry shows an otherwise competent lower esophageal sphincter with brief relaxations unrelated to swallowing. What mechanism explains reflux episodes?

A. Transient lower esophageal sphincter relaxations
B. Complete esophageal atresia
C. Universal permanent absence of sphincter tone
D. A fixed obstruction at the anal sphincter

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Transient lower esophageal sphincter relaxations

Transient relaxations unrelated to swallowing permit retrograde passage; even very small infants can have good basal sphincter tone.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has predominantly nonacid postfeed reflux. Which measurement can identify esophageal fluid irrespective of acidity?

A. A serum gastrin result alone
B. Multichannel intraluminal impedance
C. pH monitoring alone
D. A single chest radiograph

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Multichannel intraluminal impedance

Impedance detects fluid presence regardless of pH; combined sensors can characterize acid and nonacid events.

A stable infant has suspected reflux-related symptoms without a demonstrated urgent complication. Which overall approach is emphasized given treatment limitations?

A. Immediate fundoplication for nonspecific fussiness
B. Routine acid suppression for every regurgitating infant
C. Assume every NICU reflux medicine has proven safety and efficacy
D. Conservative expectant management with clinical reassessment

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Conservative expectant management with clinical reassessment

The source favors conservative management because symptoms often improve and drug or surgical safety and efficacy are limited.
