## Authored MCQ

A developing lung forms a vessel by organization of endothelial progenitors rather than sprouting from an existing vessel. Which process is occurring?

A. Vasculogenesis
B. Postnatal capillary recruitment
C. Vascular muscularization
D. Angiogenesis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Vasculogenesis

Vasculogenesis forms vessels de novo from progenitors; angiogenesis extends existing vessels.

## Authored MCQ

Most right ventricular output in a healthy fetus bypasses the lungs. Which explanation best fits this circulation?

A. Placental gas exchange permits high pulmonary resistance and low lung flow
B. Most fetal oxygen uptake occurs in ventilated alveoli
C. The fetal ductus normally carries only left-to-right flow
D. Pulmonary resistance must already be at adult levels

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Placental gas exchange permits high pulmonary resistance and low lung flow

High fetal resistance directs blood through fetal channels while the placenta provides oxygenation.

## Authored MCQ

An experimental preparation separately inflates the lungs and increases oxygen tension. What response is expected when both occur?

A. A particularly large fall in pulmonary vascular resistance
B. A sustained increase in fetal right-to-left shunting
C. A fall in pulmonary venous return
D. Maintenance of the placental circulation after cord clamping

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A particularly large fall in pulmonary vascular resistance

Inflation and oxygenation independently lower resistance, with the greatest effects together; lung flow and pulmonary venous return increase.

B. Cyclic GMP
C. Endothelin acting at smooth-muscle receptors
D. Rho kinase activity promoting calcium sensitivity
