## Authored MCQ

A stabilized preterm infant’s parent feels detached and uncertain about participating. Which supported activity can promote attachment?

A. Discouraging participation to reduce attachment
B. Supported skin-to-skin contact
C. Routine separation until discharge
D. Replacing all parent contact with photographs

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Supported skin-to-skin contact

Skin-to-skin care supports attachment and breastfeeding and can promote infant physiologic stability.

C. Repeat the abbreviations more loudly

## Authored MCQ

A NICU parent screens positive for postpartum depression. What is the appropriate interpretation?

A. The screening score alone establishes a final diagnosis
B. Further evaluation is warranted
C. All symptoms must be ordinary transient baby blues
D. No action is needed until discharge

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Further evaluation is warranted

A positive screen identifies need for evaluation and referral rather than supplying a final diagnosis.

## Authored MCQ

A postpartum parent has new delusions, confusion and disorganized behavior. Which response is supported?

A. Routine reassurance with unsupervised infant care
B. Treat the presentation as normal bonding anxiety
C. Wait for a later depression screen before acting
D. Urgent psychiatric evaluation with infant safety prioritized

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Urgent psychiatric evaluation with infant safety prioritized

Suspected postpartum psychosis warrants emergency referral; rapid fluctuation and risks to parent and infant demand safety planning.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

Parents fear managing home oxygen despite wanting their infant home. What intervention addresses the identified need?

A. Reassurance without practising equipment use
B. Skills training and supported rooming-in before discharge
C. Delay all teaching until the day after discharge
D. Interpret anxiety as proof the parents cannot learn

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Skills training and supported rooming-in before discharge

Sustained planning, practical skills and rooming-in can build confidence for home care.
