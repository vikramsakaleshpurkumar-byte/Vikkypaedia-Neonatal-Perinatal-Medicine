## Authored MCQ

A patient stopped occupational lead exposure before conception. Which reservoir can still contribute to fetal exposure?

A. New fetal synthesis of lead
B. Lead mobilized from maternal bone
C. Lead generated by placental metabolism
D. Only lead inhaled during labor

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Lead mobilized from maternal bone

Lead accumulates in bone and can be mobilized during pregnancy and transported to the fetus.

## Authored MCQ

Which mechanism makes maternal-fetal concentration gradients especially important for many environmental chemicals?

A. Exclusive transport within intact maternal erythrocytes
B. Maternal chromosome segregation
C. Fetal antibody synthesis
D. Passive diffusion

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Passive diffusion

Most xenobiotics cross by passive diffusion, with gradients and placental flow influencing fetal exposure.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A water-soluble xenobiotic enters the amniotic fluid. Which property can sustain fetal exposure?

A. Complete exclusion of water-soluble compounds
B. Guaranteed immediate clearance of every chemical
C. Conversion of all xenobiotics into harmless nutrients
D. Delayed elimination from a low-protein compartment

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Delayed elimination from a low-protein compartment

Amniotic fluid can act as a third distribution compartment with delayed elimination and low protein concentration.

</details>
