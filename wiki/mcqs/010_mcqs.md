## Authored MCQ

Deep fetal structures are difficult to visualize in a patient with substantial overlying tissue. Which trade-off guides probe selection?

A. Frequency has no effect on image quality
B. Higher frequency always improves penetration
C. Lower frequency improves penetration while higher frequency improves resolution
D. Lower frequency improves both penetration and fine resolution without trade-off

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Lower frequency improves penetration while higher frequency improves resolution

Probe choice balances lower-frequency penetration against higher-frequency spatial resolution.

## Authored MCQ

A transvaginal study clearly demonstrates a yolk sac within an intrauterine gestational sac. What has been established?

A. A normal fetal karyotype
B. An intrauterine pregnancy site
C. Absence of every additional ectopic implantation after assisted reproduction
D. A guarantee of subsequent pregnancy viability

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An intrauterine pregnancy site

A yolk sac or embryo within the uterus confirms that pregnancy site; it does not establish genetics or future outcome.
