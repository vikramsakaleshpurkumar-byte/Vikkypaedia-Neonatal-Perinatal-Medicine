## Authored MCQ

Which list correctly describes the physical modes relevant to neonatal thermal balance?

A. Ventilation, perfusion, shunting and hemolysis
B. Convection, radiation, evaporation and conduction
C. Diffusion, filtration, secretion and digestion
D. Vasoconstriction, myelination, swallowing and reflux

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Convection, radiation, evaporation and conduction

All four heat-exchange modes vary with maturity, environment and postnatal age.

## Authored MCQ

An extremely preterm infant loses substantial water and heat through the skin. Which mechanism is central?

A. A requirement for active sweating as the only source
B. An immature skin barrier
C. A fully mature impermeable stratum corneum
D. Universal excess insulating fat

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An immature skin barrier

Skin barrier competence develops with maturity, so very immature infants have much higher transepidermal water and heat loss.

## Authored MCQ

Two infants have normal core temperature, but one must greatly increase metabolism to maintain it. Which environment is thermoneutral?

A. Any environment in which temperature is briefly normal regardless of metabolic cost
B. An environment requiring maximal heat production
C. The range allowing normal temperature at minimal metabolic rate using nonevaporative adjustments
D. Only a single fixed incubator setting for all infants

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The range allowing normal temperature at minimal metabolic rate using nonevaporative adjustments

Thermoneutrality includes metabolic efficiency; a normal temperature alone does not prove it.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An extremely preterm infant has high insensible water loss in the first week. Which environmental change directly reduces evaporation?

A. Increase appropriate incubator humidity
B. Assume air temperature alone measures all water loss
C. Create condensation deliberately on incubator walls
D. Reduce humidity toward zero

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Increase appropriate incubator humidity

Higher humidity lowers evaporative loss and can aid fluid and heat balance in very immature infants.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A skin-servo incubator probe becomes detached. Which problem should prompt immediate checking of the infant and control system?

A. Overwarming from inappropriate heat delivery
B. Guaranteed automatic elimination of heat
C. Proof of infectious fever
D. A direct diagnosis of hypovolemia

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Overwarming from inappropriate heat delivery

A detached probe can lead to overwarming; device readings must be interpreted alongside infant assessment.
