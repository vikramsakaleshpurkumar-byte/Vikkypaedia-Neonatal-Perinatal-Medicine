## Authored MCQ

Which embryonic origin best describes the paired primordia that form the early heart?

A. Definitive endoderm alone
B. Surface ectoderm alone
C. Neural crest as the sole origin of myocardium
D. Lateral plate mesoderm

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Lateral plate mesoderm

Bilateral heart fields derived from lateral plate mesoderm contribute to the early tube.

## Authored MCQ

The tubular heart bends so its venous caudal portion moves dorsally and to the left of its arterial cranial portion. Which process is described?

A. Completion of all valve remodeling
B. Only coronary endothelial proliferation
C. Postnatal closure of the ductus arteriosus
D. Dextral looping

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Dextral looping

Looping repositions segments of the growing tube as chamber differentiation and septation proceed.

## Authored MCQ

Cells leave endocardial epithelium and populate extracellular matrix swellings at the AV junction and outflow tract. Which developmental structures are forming?

A. Only ventricular coronary arteries with no septal role
B. Cushions that contribute to valves and septa
C. Only fetal erythrocyte islands
D. Only the mature pericardial cavity

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Cushions that contribute to valves and septa

Endothelial-mesenchymal transition populates cardiac jelly; cushions act as primitive valves and shape later septa and valves.

B. Only pulmonary venous structures with no outflow role
C. Only red-cell maturation
D. All ventricular working myocardium as its only source

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Outflow septation and aortic arch artery development

Cardiac neural crest contributes to great-vessel walls, outflow septation and cardiac neural structures.

## Authored MCQ

Lineage studies show a clonal relationship between conduction-system cells and working cardiomyocytes. Which interpretation is supported?

A. The mature conduction system is fully formed before the heart tube exists
B. The entire conduction system must derive from circulating blood cells
C. Conduction tissue is unrelated to myocardium at every stage
D. Specialized conducting myocardium can be recruited from myocardial precursors

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Specialized conducting myocardium can be recruited from myocardial precursors

The source describes specialized myocardium arising from unspecialized myocardial cells, with signaling influencing recruitment.
