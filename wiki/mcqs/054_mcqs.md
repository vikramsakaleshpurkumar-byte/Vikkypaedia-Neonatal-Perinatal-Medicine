## Authored MCQ

A very preterm infant later shows reduced white matter and delayed myelination without a large cystic lesion. Which mechanism is emphasized?

A. Complete sparing of every axon and neuron
B. Injury and arrested maturation of pre-oligodendrocytes
C. Selective overproduction of fully mature myelin only
D. A process confined to choroid plexus epithelium

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Injury and arrested maturation of pre-oligodendrocytes

Diffuse injury includes impaired oligodendrocyte maturation and broader developmental effects.

## Authored MCQ

An inflammatory exposure precedes a later hypoxic insult in an experimental model. Which interpretation reflects the model?

A. One model proves the precise mechanism in every human infant
B. Only the final exposure can ever matter
C. Every exposure is protective regardless of timing
D. An early exposure can increase susceptibility to later injury

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An early exposure can increase susceptibility to later injury

The model considers interactions among temporally separated insults within a multifactorial process.

## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant has no cystic PVL on cranial ultrasound. Which conclusion is justified?

A. All encephalopathy of prematurity is excluded
B. MRI cannot add information about noncystic injury
C. Diffuse white matter injury is not excluded
D. A normal ultrasound guarantees normal later cognition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Diffuse white matter injury is not excluded

Ultrasound detects cystic PVL well but has limited sensitivity for diffuse noncystic injury.

PDF p. 1023
</details>

Early MRI shows bilateral periventricular diffusion restriction while conventional images are nonspecific. Which mechanism is consistent?

B. A mandatory increase in extracellular free diffusion
C. Reduced water mobility associated with cellular and axonal swelling
D. Proof that the abnormality is always chronic scar

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Acute cytotoxic and axonal swelling can lower ADC before conventional changes become clear.
