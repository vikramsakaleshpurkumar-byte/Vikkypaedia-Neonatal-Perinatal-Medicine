## Authored MCQ

A dramatic recovery motivates an ACTH trial for an eye disorder of prematurity. What did the controlled evaluation show?

A. No greater efficacy than placebo, with adverse effects
B. Reliable benefit established by the initial case alone
C. A reason to abandon all controlled comparisons
D. Clear superiority without adverse effects

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

No greater efficacy than placebo, with adverse effects

The initial response did not predict trial benefit. The placebo-controlled study found no greater efficacy and identified steroid adverse effects.

## Authored MCQ

Premature infants survived in increasingly leakproof incubators supplied with free-flow oxygen. Which unintended consequence illustrates the associated safety problem?

A. Retinopathy associated with excessive oxygen exposure
B. Evidence that survival alone captures every outcome
C. Proof that more oxygen is always safer
D. Elimination of all prematurity-related morbidity

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Retinopathy associated with excessive oxygen exposure

The chapter links high inspired oxygen concentrations in leakproof incubators to the epidemic of retrolental fibroplasia/retinopathy.

</details>
