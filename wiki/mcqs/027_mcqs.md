## Authored MCQ

A newly born infant is being assessed before the later detailed examination. Which assessment is the immediate priority?

A. A complete behavioral assessment before respiratory status
B. Only birth weight
C. Breathing, color and a brief whole-body search for major anomalies or injury
D. A guarantee that all congenital disease can be excluded

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Breathing, color and a brief whole-body search for major anomalies or injury

The primary survey checks transition, major anomalies and birth injuries across the body.

Listen to the heart and perform suitable quiet-state assessments while ensuring eventual whole-body examination

The sequence is flexible; heart, eyes, pulses and gentle hip assessment can take advantage of a quiet state.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn has no murmur and palpable femoral pulses on day 1. Which qualification matters?

A. Every serious defect must cause an immediate loud murmur
B. These findings exclude all critical congenital heart disease
C. Structural lesions cannot evolve in clinical presentation
D. A patent ductus and high right-sided pressure can mask important disease

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A patent ductus and high right-sided pressure can mask important disease

Hemodynamic transition may delay murmurs or symptoms until ductal closure and pressure changes occur.
