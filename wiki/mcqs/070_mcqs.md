## Authored MCQ

Two cohorts use different oxygen and respiratory-support criteria to classify chronic lung disease. Which caution is appropriate?

A. A support-based definition includes every severe early death
B. Definitions and local oxygen practices can change classification and predicted risk
C. Every historical definition is interchangeable
D. Oxygen use alone perfectly identifies one uniform pathology

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Definitions and local oxygen practices can change classification and predicted risk

BPD definitions evolved with treatment and survival; different criteria have limitations and can affect comparisons.

## Authored MCQ

An extremely preterm lung shows fewer simplified alveoli and abnormal sparse capillaries with little fibrosis. Which process best describes it?

A. Uniform dense fibrosis as an obligatory finding
B. An isolated acute pleural air leak
C. Impaired alveolar and vascular development
D. Complete normal alveolarization with only upper-airway noise

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Impaired alveolar and vascular development

Modern BPD often reflects arrested or disrupted development and reduced gas-exchange surface area.

## Authored MCQ

A severe chronic lung disease course raises concern for pulmonary vascular disease. Which approach is supported?

A. Use oxygen saturation only during quiet wakefulness
B. Assume the lung diagnosis excludes vascular disease
C. Early echocardiographic assessment and prevention of hypoxemia
D. Begin every vasodilator without evaluating contributing conditions

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Early echocardiographic assessment and prevention of hypoxemia

Pulmonary hypertension can complicate severe BPD; oxygenation during sleep, feeding and activity matters.

## Authored MCQ

An infant with increased work of breathing gains poorly despite fluid limitation. Which plan is supported?

A. Reduce all caloric delivery to lower respiratory effort
B. Optimize nutrient density and monitor weight, length and head growth
C. Treat growth failure as unrelated to lung recovery
D. Assess only fluid volume and never nutrient content

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Optimize nutrient density and monitor weight, length and head growth

Increased demand and interrupted feeding can impair growth; malnutrition can delay alveolar development and weaning.

## Authored MCQ

A team proposes prolonged steroid therapy after a transient improvement in mechanics. Which principle fits the chapter?

A. Use clearly defined goals after assessing benefits and adverse effects
B. Apply one regimen to all BPD phenotypes without reassessment
C. Assume short-term improvement proves long-term respiratory benefit
D. Continue indefinitely because inhaled or systemic steroids lack risks

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Use clearly defined goals after assessing benefits and adverse effects

Steroids can have short-term anti-inflammatory effects, but long-term respiratory outcomes in established BPD are not established and adverse effects matter.
