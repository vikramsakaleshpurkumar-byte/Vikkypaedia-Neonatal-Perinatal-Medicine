## Authored MCQ

Which function explains why amniotic fluid is more than a marker on a scan?

A. Make fetal swallowing unnecessary
B. Replace placental oxygen exchange
C. Prevent every congenital anomaly
D. Cushion fetus and cord while permitting movement and structural development

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Cushion fetus and cord while permitting movement and structural development

Fluid protects against trauma and permits mobility needed for growth and development.

A fetus with bilateral renal agenesis has almost no amniotic fluid later in pregnancy. Which mechanism is most relevant?

A. Excess fetal urinary output
B. Increased fetal swallowing as the only possible cause
C. A requirement for maternal water deprivation
D. Loss of the major fetal urinary contribution

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Loss of the major fetal urinary contribution

Fetal renal output is a fundamental source of fluid, especially in the second half of pregnancy.

## Authored MCQ

A clinician needs repeated practical estimates of amniotic fluid. Why is ultrasound used instead of direct measurement or dye dilution?

A. Dye dilution never requires sampling
B. Ultrasound directly collects every milliliter of fluid
C. Direct measurement is routinely repeatable without delivery
D. Direct measurement and dye dilution are cumbersome or invasive

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Direct measurement and dye dilution are cumbersome or invasive

Direct measurement is largely limited to cesarean delivery, and dye dilution requires invasive sampling and laboratory support.

## Authored MCQ

A fetus with an obstructive swallowing problem develops excess fluid. Which pathway best explains the finding?

A. A mechanism requiring normal swallowing in every case
B. Reduced absorption/removal of amniotic fluid
C. Increased absorption through swallowing
D. Complete cessation of urine as the required cause

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Reduced absorption/removal of amniotic fluid

Reduced fetal swallowing limits fluid removal and can produce polyhydramnios; overproduction and idiopathic cases also occur.

</details>
