## Authored MCQ

Which pair correctly classifies neonatal electronic data?

A. Every electronic record is inherently structured
B. Coded gestational age: structured; free-text note: unstructured
C. An image becomes structured solely because it is digital
D. Free-text note: structured; coded birth weight: unstructured

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Coded gestational age: structured; free-text note: unstructured

Structured data use predefined fields or coding; free text, pictures and audio are examples of unstructured information.

## Authored MCQ

After clinicians are taught to distinguish spontaneous intestinal perforation from NEC, the recorded frequency of perforation rises. Which explanation must be considered?

A. Proof that the teaching caused bowel injury
B. Elimination of all classification error
C. Ascertainment bias from changed identification
D. Proof of a new biologic epidemic

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Ascertainment bias from changed identification

The chapter uses this example to show how changing recognition and classification can produce an apparent incidence increase.

## Authored MCQ

Investigators want to study rare potential harms for which a randomized trial would need an impractical sample. What is a reasonable database role?

A. Generate and investigate hypotheses while addressing bias
B. Guarantee causality without other evidence
C. Ignore exposure definitions because the outcome is rare
D. Make every randomized trial unnecessary

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Generate and investigate hypotheses while addressing bias

Large observational datasets can investigate questions difficult to randomize, but their design and bias limitations remain.

## Authored MCQ

A network evaluates an antibiotic stewardship initiative over time. Which database feature is especially useful?

A. Removing all comparison data after implementation
B. Only recording the final favorable result
C. Changing outcome definitions at each hospital without documentation
D. Consistent process and outcome definitions

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Consistent process and outcome definitions

Pre-existing, consistent measures allow services to track change and collaborate on quality improvement.
