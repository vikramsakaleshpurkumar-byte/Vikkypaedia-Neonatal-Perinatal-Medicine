## Authored MCQ

Which respiratory change allows sustained postnatal gas exchange?

A. Delivering maximal tidal volumes regardless of gestation
B. Replacement of lung liquid by air and establishment of FRC
C. Keeping all alveoli permanently liquid filled
D. Eliminating pulmonary blood flow

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Replacement of lung liquid by air and establishment of FRC

Lung aeration and FRC support gas exchange while pulmonary blood flow increases.

## Authored MCQ

A delivery setting has no compressed gas source. Which device can still provide positive-pressure inflations?

A. Self-inflating bag
B. T-piece device requiring continuous flow
C. Flow-inflating bag without gas flow
D. A disconnected oxygen blender alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Self-inflating bag

Self-inflating bags re-expand after compression and can operate without a gas supply.

## Authored MCQ

A continuous-flow T-piece is set up. What action produces the peak inflation pressure?

A. Occlude the T-piece opening
B. Disconnect the gas supply
C. Remove the pressure-limiting system permanently
D. Squeeze a self-refilling reservoir that is not present

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Occlude the T-piece opening

Occluding the opening delivers set peak pressure; leaving it open permits PEEP or CPAP.

PDF p. 562
</details>

## Authored MCQ

Which fit supports a seal while avoiding an excessively large mask?

A. The whole face and lower neck
B. Mouth and nose without covering eyes or extending beyond the chin
C. Nose only with the mouth freely open in every case
D. Eyes and forehead only

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Mouth and nose without covering eyes or extending beyond the chin

Correct size and positioning over mouth and nose help limit leaks; eyes and chin should not be overlapped.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A surfactant-deficient lung repeatedly collapses at end expiration. What is the intended benefit of PEEP?

A. Replace assessment of delivered ventilation
B. Remove all functional residual capacity
C. Maintain end-expiratory volume and reduce distal airway collapse
D. Guarantee benefit on every long-term outcome

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Maintain end-expiratory volume and reduce distal airway collapse

PEEP helps maintain FRC and reduces end-expiratory collapse; physiologic rationale does not prove every clinical outcome.

PDF p. 568
</details>
