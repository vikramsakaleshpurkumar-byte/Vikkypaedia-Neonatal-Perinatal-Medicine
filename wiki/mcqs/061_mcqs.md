## Authored MCQ

A probe records a sound generated in response to stimulation of the cochlea. Which structure produces the measured response?

A. Only middle-ear muscles
B. Only the eighth-nerve conduction pathway
C. Outer hair cells
D. Only auditory cortex

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Outer hair cells

OAE reflects outer hair-cell function; outer or middle-ear obstruction can interfere with measurement.

## Authored MCQ

An infant passes OAE but fails auditory brainstem response testing. Which explanation is plausible?

A. Proof that all auditory pathways are normal
B. OAE directly confirming normal brainstem conduction
C. Neural hearing loss with preserved outer hair-cell function
D. A mandatory error in the ABR because OAE is comprehensive

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Neural hearing loss with preserved outer hair-cell function

OAE can miss neural disease involving inner hair cells, auditory nerve or brainstem pathways.

PDF p. 1159
</details>

## Authored MCQ

A newborn fails screening while ear-canal debris and middle-ear fluid are present. Which response is appropriate?

A. Arrange timely follow-up because transient conductive interference can cause failure
B. Wait until school age to obtain a diagnostic assessment
C. Dismiss the result permanently because fluid is present
D. Declare permanent profound deafness from the screen alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Arrange timely follow-up because transient conductive interference can cause failure

A screening result requires follow-up; transient outer or middle-ear dysfunction can cause false-positive failure.

PDF p. 1159
</details>

## Authored MCQ

An infant is diagnosed with unilateral hearing loss. Which plan fits the source recommendations?

A. Wait until speech delay becomes severe
B. Refer to early intervention at diagnosis, including unilateral loss
C. Refer only if both ears have profound loss
D. Rely only on later classroom accommodations

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Refer to early intervention at diagnosis, including unilateral loss

Early individualized family-centered services are recommended for unilateral and bilateral hearing loss.

PDF p. 1161
</details>

## Authored MCQ

A child who passed newborn screening later shows language delay. Which next step is supported?

A. Repeat only an unrelated visual screening
B. Exclude hearing loss forever based on the newborn result
C. Assume all delay is behavioral without assessment
D. Audiologic and speech-language assessment because delayed or progressive loss can occur

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Audiologic and speech-language assessment because delayed or progressive loss can occur

Mild loss may be missed and some causes, including CMV, can produce delayed or progressive hearing loss.

PDF p. 1165
</details>
