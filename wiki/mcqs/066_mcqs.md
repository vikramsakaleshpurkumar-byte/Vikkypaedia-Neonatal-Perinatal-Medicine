## Authored MCQ

A spontaneously breathing preterm infant improves on nasal CPAP. Which mechanism best explains the effect?

A. Maintenance of end-expiratory lung volume and airway patency
B. Mandatory inflation of the stomach as the therapeutic goal
C. Replacement of every deficient surfactant molecule
D. Elimination of all spontaneous respiratory effort

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Maintenance of end-expiratory lung volume and airway patency

CPAP prevents end-expiratory collapse, supports FRC and can stent the upper airway.

## Authored MCQ

A ventilator adjusts pressure using measured delivered tidal volume. Which goal does this describe?

A. Guarantee zero leak around every uncuffed tube
B. Hold pressure fixed while ignoring volume changes
C. Deliver adult tidal volumes to every newborn
D. Keep tidal volume within a target range despite changing mechanics

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Keep tidal volume within a target range despite changing mechanics

Hybrid systems use feedback to adjust delivery toward a volume target; neonatal leaks and measurement limitations still matter.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A ventilated infant’s mechanics slow lung emptying but expiratory time remains short. What complication becomes more likely?

A. Gas trapping with incomplete exhalation
B. Complete emptying guaranteed by each cycle
C. Elimination of alveolar rupture risk
D. A mandatory decrease in lung volume to zero

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Gas trapping with incomplete exhalation

Insufficient time relative to the resistance-compliance time constant leaves gas behind and can cause overinflation.

PDF p. 1250
</details>

## Authored MCQ

A ventilated infant suddenly becomes hypoxemic and hypotensive with markedly reduced breath sounds on one side. What emergency should be considered?

A. A benign unchanged ventilator waveform
B. Stable atelectasis requiring no urgent assessment
C. Tension pneumothorax
D. Only uncomplicated feeding intolerance

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Tension pneumothorax

Pleural air under pressure can impair ventilation and circulation and requires prompt treatment.

PDF p. 1266
</details>

## Authored MCQ

One region is repeatedly overdistended while another cycles between closure and reopening. Which paired mechanisms apply?

A. Only oxygen toxicity in both regions
B. Only infection in both regions
C. Volutrauma and atelectrauma, respectively
D. Atelectrauma and volutrauma, respectively

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Volutrauma and atelectrauma, respectively

Volutrauma is overdistention injury; atelectrauma follows repetitive recruitment and derecruitment.

PDF p. 1267
</details>
