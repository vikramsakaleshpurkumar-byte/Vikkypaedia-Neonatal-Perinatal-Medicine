## Authored MCQ

A fetal surveillance test is abnormal. Which general interpretation is most justified?

A. An abnormal test identifies a specific cause with certainty
B. False-positive results are possible and clinical context matters
C. Every abnormal result proves irreversible fetal injury
D. Surveillance predicts every possible stillbirth mechanism

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

False-positive results are possible and clinical context matters

Tests reflect biophysical correlates of hypoxemia and have false-positive results; not all causes of stillbirth are predictable.

## Authored MCQ

At a gestation where the standard 15-by-15 criterion applies, which tracing meets the stated reactivity criterion?

A. One acceleration of 5 beats/min lasting 5 seconds
B. At least two accelerations of 15 beats/min lasting at least 15 seconds
C. One prolonged deceleration lasting 2 minutes
D. A flat baseline with no accelerations

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

At least two accelerations of 15 beats/min lasting at least 15 seconds

The described reactive NST has at least two accelerations peaking 15 beats/min above baseline and lasting at least 15 seconds.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An ultrasound finding partially meets a BPP component criterion. Which scoring rule is used?

A. Each component receives any score from 0 to 5
B. Each component receives either 0 or 2 points
C. Only the NST contributes points
D. Every incomplete component receives 1 point

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Each component receives either 0 or 2 points

The five components are assigned 0 or 2 points, yielding an even total between 0 and 10.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A four-quadrant fluid assessment yields an AFI of 4 cm. Which interpretation follows the stated criterion?

A. An uninterpretable number because AFI uses only one pocket
B. Normal fluid by the AFI criterion
C. Polyhydramnios
D. Oligohydramnios

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Oligohydramnios

The source defines oligohydramnios by AFI of 5 cm or less, or no single vertical pocket greater than 2 cm.

## Authored MCQ

During oxytocin infusion, contractions become too frequent and fetal recovery between contractions is impaired. Which intervention targets that mechanism?

A. Ignore contraction frequency when interpreting the tracing
B. Reduce or discontinue oxytocin as clinically indicated
C. Increase oxytocin to shorten recovery intervals further
D. Use maternal oxygen solely for fetal well-being as the routine corrective measure

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Reduce or discontinue oxytocin as clinically indicated

Resolving tachysystole can improve fetal status; excessive contraction frequency limits recovery.

PDF p. 240
</details>
