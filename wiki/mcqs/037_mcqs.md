A unit makes treatment decisions from apnea and desaturation alarms. Which skill improves interpretation?

A. Assume adult sensor validation establishes neonatal accuracy
B. Treat every alarm as a confirmed diagnosis
C. Ignore fragile-skin risks
D. Recognize device function, artifact and true physiologic events

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Recognize device function, artifact and true physiologic events

The source emphasizes safe accurate acquisition and user understanding to avoid nuisance alarms and distortion of real events.

## Authored MCQ

An extremely preterm infant needs continuous ECG monitoring. Which practice-related concern is relevant?

A. A need to remove the skin barrier to improve physiology
B. Skin injury and increased transepidermal water loss
C. A guarantee that adhesives are harmless at every gestation
D. ECG electrodes measuring arterial oxygen directly

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Skin injury and increased transepidermal water loss

Repeated applications can impair adhesion and signal and injure fragile skin, increasing water loss.

## Authored MCQ

The arterial transducer has been positioned above the infant’s heart. What bias can this introduce?

A. An erroneously low blood-pressure reading
B. An obligatorily high reading
C. A direct measurement of intracranial pressure
D. No possible measurement error

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An erroneously low blood-pressure reading

Incorrect elevation can lower the displayed arterial pressure; proper leveling and zeroing are required.

## Authored MCQ

Which signal component is used to distinguish pulsatile arterial blood from relatively constant tissue absorption?

A. Heartbeat-related pulsatile light absorption
B. Direct measurement of every dissolved oxygen molecule
C. Only the constant skin pigment signal
D. The infant’s weight alone

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Heartbeat-related pulsatile light absorption

Ratios of pulsatile to constant red and infrared signals estimate the fraction of oxygenated arterial hemoglobin.

## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant has very low tidal volumes. Which feature of conventional sidestream sampling may reduce accuracy?

A. Guaranteed perfect agreement despite airway leaks
B. Direct invasive measurement of arterial CO2 without gas collection
C. Constant aspiration of expired gas relative to small available volumes
D. Complete absence of any gas sampling

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Constant aspiration of expired gas relative to small available volumes

Sampling requirements can be problematic at very low tidal volumes; microstream approaches use smaller samples.

</details>
