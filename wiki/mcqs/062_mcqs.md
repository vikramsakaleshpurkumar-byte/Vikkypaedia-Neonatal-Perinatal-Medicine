## Authored MCQ

A high-risk infant shows reduced variability of spontaneous movements. Which assessment addresses this pattern?

A. An adult intelligence test
B. Prechtl General Movement Assessment for neuromotor risk
C. A serum drug level as a direct movement classification
D. A hearing screen as the sole motor assessment

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Prechtl General Movement Assessment for neuromotor risk

General movement quality can help identify risk of neuromotor deficits, including spastic cerebral palsy.

## Authored MCQ

A preterm toddler with motor and sensory impairments screens positive on an autism checklist. What is the appropriate interpretation?

A. All positive results should be ignored as false positives
B. The checklist alone establishes a definitive diagnosis
C. Prematurity makes autism impossible
D. Further evaluation should consider coexisting impairments and the full developmental profile

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Further evaluation should consider coexisting impairments and the full developmental profile

The source notes substantial coexisting disability among screen-positive preterm children; screening supports evaluation rather than replacing it.
