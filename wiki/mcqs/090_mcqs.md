## Authored MCQ

A newborn has discordant glucose results from different sample methods. Which explanation should be considered?

A. A delayed room-temperature sample always gives a falsely high value
B. Red cells cannot consume glucose after collection
C. Sample type has no influence at low values
D. Plasma and whole-blood values differ and delayed handling permits glycolysis

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Plasma and whole-blood values differ and delayed handling permits glycolysis

Whole-blood and plasma values differ; ongoing cellular glycolysis can lower an inadequately handled sample result.

Which newborn has a recognized reason for glucose monitoring even if currently asymptomatic?

A. An infant of a diabetic mother
B. Every healthy term infant solely because of normal transition
C. No infant unless a laboratory diagnosis is already confirmed
D. Only infants already having seizures

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An infant of a diabetic mother

Infants of diabetic mothers, growth abnormalities, prematurity and acute illness are among monitored risk groups.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn has recurrent profound hypoglycemia with inappropriately persistent insulin action and low ketones. What makes the pattern dangerous?

A. Insulin provides abundant ketones as a protective fuel
B. Low ketones guarantee normal cerebral energy delivery
C. Persistent episodes are always normal transitional physiology
D. Insulin restricts endogenous glucose release and alternative fuel availability

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Insulin restricts endogenous glucose release and alternative fuel availability

Excess insulin inhibits glycogenolysis and availability of alternate fuels, increasing the risk of brain injury.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

An extremely premature infant has high glucose while receiving an apparently appropriate infusion. Which mechanism is described?

A. A result that cannot be influenced by sepsis or medicines
B. Automatic proof of autoimmune diabetes
C. Persistent endogenous glucose production with immature insulin regulation
D. A necessary absence of all stress hormones

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Persistent endogenous glucose production with immature insulin regulation

Prematurity, stress, endogenous production and altered insulin effects contribute; medications and sepsis may also be relevant.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has persistent early diabetes and an activating KCNJ11 or ABCC8 mutation. Which mechanism fits?

A. Loss of intestinal glucose absorption alone
B. Mandatory autoimmune beta-cell destruction
C. Excess insulin secretion causing recurrent hypoglycemia
D. Failure of channel closure impairs insulin secretion

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Failure of channel closure impairs insulin secretion

Activating channel mutations prevent normal closure and inhibit beta-cell insulin release.
