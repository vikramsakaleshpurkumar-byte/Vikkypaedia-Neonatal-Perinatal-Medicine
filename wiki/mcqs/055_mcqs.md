## Authored MCQ

A very preterm infant has a new subependymal hemorrhage near the caudate. Which site is characteristic?

A. Only the spinal epidural space
B. Caudothalamic germinal matrix
C. Only the mature cortical sulci
D. Only the extracranial subgaleal space

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Caudothalamic germinal matrix

The germinal matrix around the caudate is a common origin in preterm infants.

## Authored MCQ

An early scan detects intraventricular blood. Which monitoring plan is supported?

A. Use head appearance alone to exclude progression
B. Wait for seizures before reassessing ventricular size
C. One scan permanently defines the maximal extent
D. Serial cranial ultrasound to assess evolution and ventricular dilatation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Serial cranial ultrasound to assess evolution and ventricular dilatation

Serial ultrasound permits detection, grading and monitoring of posthemorrhagic ventricular dilatation.

## Authored MCQ

An otherwise relatively well term newborn develops focal clonic seizures in the first days. Which diagnosis should be considered?

A. Only generalized metabolic disease with no need for imaging
B. Physiologic tremor proven solely by focality
C. Perinatal arterial ischemic stroke
D. A normal developmental movement by definition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Perinatal arterial ischemic stroke

PAIS often presents with focal clonic seizures and warrants appropriate neuroimaging.
