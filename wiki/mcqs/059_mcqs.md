## Authored MCQ

A preterm infant’s head growth is being followed. Which approach best supports interpretation?

A. Use visual estimation instead of a tape
B. Use an adult head-size threshold
C. Measure maximal occipitofrontal circumference and plot on an age-appropriate preterm chart
D. Interpret one measurement without gestational age or trend

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Measure maximal occipitofrontal circumference and plot on an age-appropriate preterm chart

Consistent occipitofrontal measurement and age-adjusted growth charts help assess intracranial growth.

## Authored MCQ

A quiet infant has sustained bulging of the anterior fontanelle. Which interpretation is appropriate?

A. Assume all fullness is caused by crying
B. Treat it as proof of a specific organism
C. Consider an intracranial process with increased pressure
D. Use it alone to quantify ventricular size

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Consider an intracranial process with increased pressure

Sustained bulging can indicate raised intracranial pressure; crying-related transient fullness is different.

## Authored MCQ

An infant’s head circumference is above the expected range. Which differential is supported?

A. Only scalp molding regardless of trajectory
B. A normal brain volume proven by head size alone
C. Enlarged brain, abnormal intracranial structures or hydrocephalus
D. Hydrocephalus as the only possible cause

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Enlarged brain, abnormal intracranial structures or hydrocephalus

The chapter distinguishes increased brain volume, masses or vascular lesions, and enlarged CSF spaces.

## Authored MCQ

A cranial suture fuses prematurely. Which pattern explains the resulting head shape?

A. Reduced growth only parallel to the suture with no compensation
B. Uniform unrestricted growth at every suture
C. Restricted growth perpendicular to the fused suture with compensatory growth elsewhere
D. No change in shape unless all sutures fuse

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Restricted growth perpendicular to the fused suture with compensatory growth elsewhere

Failure of perpendicular growth and compensation at unfused sites produce characteristic patterns.

PDF p. 1143
</details>

## Authored MCQ

Viewed from above, an infant has a parallelogram-shaped head, unilateral occipital flattening and forward displacement of the same-side ear. Which diagnosis is favored?

A. Sagittal craniosynostosis established by this pattern
B. Hydranencephaly established by ear position
C. Lambdoid craniosynostosis
D. Positional plagiocephaly

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Positional plagiocephaly

Forward ipsilateral ear displacement and a parallelogram pattern favor positional deformity; lambdoid synostosis tends to displace the ear posteriorly.

PDF p. 1145
</details>
