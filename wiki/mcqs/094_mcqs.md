## Authored MCQ

A critically ill newborn has no similarly affected relatives. Which interpretation is appropriate?

A. An inherited metabolic disorder remains possible
B. Recessive disease requires an affected parent
C. Every genetic disorder must recur in an older sibling
D. A negative family history eliminates X-linked disease

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

An inherited metabolic disorder remains possible

Most affected neonates may lack similarly affected relatives; recessive inheritance and new variants help explain this.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn with metabolic deterioration has a positive blood culture. What should happen to an otherwise indicated metabolic evaluation?

A. Cancel it because bacteremia excludes inherited disease
B. Continue it because infection and metabolic disease can coexist
C. Assume every metabolic abnormality is a harmless culture artifact
D. Restrict the differential to a single infectious mechanism

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Continue it because infection and metabolic disease can coexist

The chapter specifically notes that metabolic disorders can predispose to sepsis, including galactosemia with E. coli infection.

An ill newborn has unexplained encephalopathy. Which rapid laboratory set most directly helps identify a metabolic phenotype?

The source core screening set uses these tests to identify glucose, acid-base, energy and nitrogen-disposal disturbances.

## Authored MCQ

A newborn has metabolic acidosis and a suspected inherited overproduction of organic acids. Which principle should guide treatment?

A. Ignore shock, kidney and liver disease in the differential
B. Assume bicarbonate alone removes the underlying disorder
C. Regard all neonatal acidosis as the same inherited disease
D. Identify and treat the cause while supporting physiology

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Identify and treat the cause while supporting physiology

The source emphasizes cause-directed treatment and assessment of common systemic causes as well as inborn errors.

## Authored MCQ

A previously well newborn develops poor feeding, lethargy, vomiting and tachypnea over the first days, then rapidly worsening consciousness. Which test should be urgently included?

A. Urine culture alone with delayed metabolic review
B. Transaminases alone before considering ammonia
C. An elective amino-acid panel without immediate ammonia measurement
D. Plasma ammonia with appropriate specimen handling

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Plasma ammonia with appropriate specimen handling

Hyperammonemia may progress rapidly to coma and irreversible neurologic injury; immediate investigation is essential.
