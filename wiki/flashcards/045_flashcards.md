<details><summary>Which drug properties favor placental diffusion?</summary>

Small, lipid-soluble, largely unionized and weakly protein-bound. These properties favor movement across placental membranes by passive diffusion.

A larger volume of distribution per kilogram. Newborns have relatively more total and extracellular body water, increasing distribution of hydrophilic drugs.

Delayed CYP1A2 activity. The primary caffeine demethylation pathway involves CYP1A2, which develops slowly after birth.
