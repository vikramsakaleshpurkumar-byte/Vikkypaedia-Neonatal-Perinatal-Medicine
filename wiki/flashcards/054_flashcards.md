<details><summary>What white matter injury can be missed by ultrasound?</summary>

Diffuse white matter injury is not excluded. Ultrasound detects cystic PVL well but has limited sensitivity for diffuse noncystic injury.

<details><summary>How may ADC change during acute white matter injury?</summary>

Reduced water mobility associated with cellular and axonal swelling. Acute cytotoxic and axonal swelling can lower ADC before conventional changes become clear.

</details>

<details><summary>Does DEHSI alone establish major developmental impairment?</summary>

The isolated signal finding does not establish significant developmental delay. The source distinguishes DEHSI alone from combinations including white matter loss and stresses limitations of prediction.

</details>

<details><summary>Why is encephalopathy of prematurity broader than focal PVL?</summary>

It includes diffuse white matter injury, neuronal and axonal involvement and altered maturation of interconnected gray and white matter systems.
