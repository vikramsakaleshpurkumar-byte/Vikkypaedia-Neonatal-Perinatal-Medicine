<details><summary>Which urine metabolites are commonly used in neuroblastoma assessment?</summary>

Homovanillic acid and vanillylmandelic acid. Many neuroblastomas secrete catecholamines; urinary HVA and VMA are commonly used diagnostic tests.

<details><summary>Why does neonatal AFP require age-adjusted interpretation?</summary>

AFP is physiologically high at birth and must be assessed against age-appropriate values. Normal AFP is high at birth and declines over infancy; tumor assessment requires developmental context and the broader findings.

</details>
