<details><summary>Does situs solitus guarantee normal atrioventricular and ventriculoarterial connections?</summary>

Normal visceral arrangement does not guarantee normal cardiac connections. Situs describes lateralization; concordance or discordance of cardiac connections requires separate evaluation.

<details><summary>What findings suggest a vein of Galen malformation in a neonate with heart failure?</summary>

Vein of Galen aneurysmal malformation. A large cerebral arteriovenous shunt can divert systemic flow and produce high-output failure, a bruit and hydrocephalus.

<details><summary>What course is described for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in infants of diabetic mothers?</summary>

Often resolves during the first six months. The chapter describes an excellent prognosis with complete resolution within the first six months for this specific form.

<details><summary>Why can a small rapidly developing effusion be dangerous?</summary>

Rapid accumulation causing cardiac tamponade. Rapid accumulation can raise intrapericardial pressure and impair filling even when fluid volume is small; central lines are a common iatrogenic cause.

</details>

<details><summary>What symptom combination should prompt consideration of a vascular ring?</summary>

Vascular ring or sling. Vascular rings may compress the airway and esophagus, producing combined respiratory and feeding symptoms.

</details>
