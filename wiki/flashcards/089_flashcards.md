<details><summary>What are important limitations of radiographic pneumatosis assessment?</summary>

Stool may mimic pneumatosis and absence of clear pneumatosis does not exclude disease. The source describes both misinterpretation of stool and disease progression without classic gas findings.

<details><summary>What are the principal initial medical measures for suspected NEC?</summary>

Stop enteral feeds, decompress the gut, provide antibiotics and support physiology. Medical management targets bowel rest, decompression, infection coverage and metabolic, hematologic, respiratory and circulatory support.

</details>

<details><summary>Why is human milk relevant to NEC prevention?</summary>

It is associated with less NEC and provides immune and barrier-supporting components. Human milk is associated with reduced NEC and has multiple protective bioactive constituents; risk is not eliminated.
