<details><summary>What does environmental mismatch mean in DOHaD?</summary>

Mismatch between early developmental cues and later environment. A larger disparity between early adaptation and the later environment can increase disease risk.

DNA or histone modification affecting gene regulation. Epigenetic modifications can link environmental exposures with stable changes in gene expression.

Balance adequate brain energy with potential nutritional excess. The source emphasizes both adequate developing-brain nutrition and avoidance of excess, with long-term follow-up.

<details><summary>What distinguishes an epidemiologic association from a deterministic prediction in developmental origins?</summary>

Population associations identify elevated risks and hypotheses; they do not establish that every exposed individual will develop a particular adult disease.
