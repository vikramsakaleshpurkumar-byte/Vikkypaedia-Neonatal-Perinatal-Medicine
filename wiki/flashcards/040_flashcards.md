<details><summary>Why should survival and neurodevelopment be discussed separately in periviable counseling?</summary>

Improved survival does not imply absence of later impairment. The source reports progress in survival alongside persistent developmental morbidity, requiring separate outcome discussion.

Review the accuracy and uncertainty of gestational dating. Outcomes vary within the periviable period and ultrasound dating retains uncertainty, making accuracy relevant to counseling.

<details><summary>Which losses inform fluid planning for a periviable infant?</summary>

Adjust to individual insensible loss, urine output and weight trends while avoiding overload. The source emphasizes substantial losses despite humidity and the risks associated with fluid overload.

<details><summary>Why can the most immature myocardium respond poorly to preload and afterload changes?</summary>

Immature calcium handling, myocardial structure and contractile/diastolic function limit adaptation and produce a relatively flat Frank-Starling response.
