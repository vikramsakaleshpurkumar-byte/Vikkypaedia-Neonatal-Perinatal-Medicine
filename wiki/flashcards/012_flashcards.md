<details><summary>Why is maternal risk central to fetal-surgery consent?</summary>

Maternal procedural risks and available alternatives. Anesthesia, surgery and continued pregnancy expose the mother to risks, so counseling must cover these and alternatives.

Uterine rupture risk requiring cesarean delivery in future pregnancies. The source describes mandatory cesarean delivery after fetal-surgery hysterotomy because of rupture risk.

<details><summary>What physiologic support is maintained during EXIT?</summary>

EXIT with uteroplacental circulation maintained. EXIT maintains uteroplacental circulation during the fetal intervention and is followed by delivery once the airway is established.

<details><summary>Why is maternal hemorrhage a particular concern during EXIT?</summary>

Uterine relaxation is necessary during the intervention but increases hemorrhage risk; coordinated anesthesia and surgical management is essential.
