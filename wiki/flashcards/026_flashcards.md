<details><summary>How do predominant pain mechanisms change across labor stages?</summary>

Visceral predominance first, somatic predominance second. First-stage cervical and lower uterine distention is predominantly visceral; pelvic and perineal distention adds somatic pain in the second stage.

Consider placental transfer plus drug properties, dose and route. Lipid barrier crossing suggests transfer potential, while pharmacokinetics, dose and administration affect actual fetal exposure.
