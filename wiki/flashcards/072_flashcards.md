Dextral looping. Looping repositions segments of the growing tube as chamber differentiation and septation proceed.

<details><summary>What is the role of endocardial cushions in heart development?</summary>

Cushions that contribute to valves and septa. Endothelial-mesenchymal transition populates cardiac jelly; cushions act as primitive valves and shape later septa and valves.

</details>

Outflow septation and aortic arch artery development. Cardiac neural crest contributes to great-vessel walls, outflow septation and cardiac neural structures.

Specialized conducting myocardium can be recruited from myocardial precursors. The source describes specialized myocardium arising from unspecialized myocardial cells, with signaling influencing recruitment.
