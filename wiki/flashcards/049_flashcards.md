<details><summary>Which immunoglobulin class is actively transported across placenta?</summary>

IgG. Placental Fc receptors actively transport maternal IgG, especially late in pregnancy.

<details><summary>What may elevated cord IgM indicate?</summary>

Fetal antigen exposure and antibody synthesis, while considering maternal-fetal bleeding. IgM does not normally cross placenta; elevation can reflect fetal synthesis, but total IgM is not pathogen-specific and bleeding can complicate interpretation.

PDF p. 856
</details>

<details><summary>Which complement pathways can activate without specific antibody?</summary>

Alternative and lectin pathways. Alternative activation can occur on surfaces and lectin activation follows recognition of microbial carbohydrates.

<details><summary>Which antibody predominates in human milk?</summary>

Secretory IgA. Secretory IgA predominates and its secretory component supports resistance to gastrointestinal digestion.

<details><summary>What infection patterns raise concern for a host-defense defect?</summary>

An immune defect alongside other predisposing conditions. Unusual, recurrent or incompletely treated infections and associated growth problems warrant investigation of host defenses.

</details>

It binds iron, limiting availability of a growth cofactor to susceptible bacteria.
