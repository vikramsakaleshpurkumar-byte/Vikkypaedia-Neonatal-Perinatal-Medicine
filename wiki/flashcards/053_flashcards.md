Later neurons occupy progressively more superficial layers. After early deep layers form, later neurons pass them toward superficial cortical positions.

<details><summary>How do agyria and pachygyria differ?</summary>

Agyria and pachygyria, respectively. Agyria is lack of gyration; pachygyria describes reduced broad gyri with shallow sulci in the lissencephaly spectrum.
