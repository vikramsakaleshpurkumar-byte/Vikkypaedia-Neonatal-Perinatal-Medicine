Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis. Progressive nonbilious projectile emesis in early infancy with contraction alkalosis is the classic pattern.

<details><summary>What developmental mechanism is associated with intrinsic duodenal atresia or stenosis?</summary>

Failure of recanalization after epithelial proliferation. The embryonic duodenum transiently becomes solid; failure of recanalization is linked to intrinsic atresia or stenosis.

<details><summary>Why can malrotation predispose to ischemic bowel injury?</summary>

Twisting of the mesentery compromising bowel blood supply. Improper fixation permits twisting around the mesenteric vascular axis and can compromise perfusion.

<details><summary>What mechanism produces obstruction in Hirschsprung disease?</summary>

Arrested development of the myenteric nervous system. Congenital intestinal aganglionosis produces a functionally obstructed distal segment.
