A constant fraction eliminated per unit time. First-order elimination removes a constant proportion over time, producing an exponential concentration decrease.

<details><summary>Why can normal early creatinine be misleading in acute kidney injury?</summary>

Renal clearance may already be reduced before creatinine rises. Creatinine takes time to accumulate, so falling filtration can precede a measurable rise.
