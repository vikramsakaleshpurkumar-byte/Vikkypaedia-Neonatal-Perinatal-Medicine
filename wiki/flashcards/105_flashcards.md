<details><summary>How is bilirubin converted from mg/dL to micromoles/L in the conversion table?</summary>

171.04 micromoles/L. Multiply the mg/dL value by 17.104: 10 × 17.104 = 171.04 micromoles/L. This is a unit conversion, not a treatment threshold.

<details><summary>How do atomic weight and valence determine milliequivalents?</summary>

2 mEq. mEq = mg × valence / atomic weight; 40 × 2 / 40 = 2. This calculation concerns elemental calcium, not a calcium-salt dose.

</details>

<details><summary>How many micrograms equal one milligram?</summary>

1000 micrograms equal 1 milligram: milli denotes 10^-3 and micro denotes 10^-6 of the base unit. Preserve these prefixes explicitly in all calculations.
